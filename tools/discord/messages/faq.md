# FAQ

**Do I need a new save?**
No. The art shop's letter arrives the morning after you install.

**The mod doesn't load.**
Check you have SMAPI 4.0+, Content Patcher 2.9+ and SpaceCore, and that both folders inside `PelicanPalette` (`PelicanPalette` and `[CP] Pelican Palette`) are in your `Mods` folder. Still stuck? Post in {#help} with your SMAPI log from <https://smapi.io/log>.

**I can't start a painting.**
Read the line under **Begin**. It tells you what's missing: a canvas of that size, a Palette in your bag (not needed on Simplified), or the time, since a painting can't run past 2am.

**A colour on the palette is faded.**
You don't know it yet. Buy its pigment from Sable, pick up a tube, or grind it in a Pigment Mill.

**My paint keeps running out.**
Buy tubes of the colours you use most, or grind your own in a Pigment Mill. Tubes refill the Palette when you sit down at the easel.

**My paintings never get past gold.**
Iridium needs Painting level 6, a high score, and usually Inspired or flawless mixing.

---

**Who can I paint?**
Any friend at 4 hearts or more, from medium canvases. Also any monster you've slain, your pet, your horse and your farm animals.

**How do I paint the view?**
Place an easel outdoors and use it, or use the one in the art shop's yard.

**How do I meet Margot?**
Find a damaged old painting (your first is guaranteed from Painting level 3, in the next artifact spot or fishing treasure chest) and take it to Gunther at the museum. Margot arrives a week after his letter.

**How do I restore old paintings myself?**
Reach Painting level 10 and keep painting until Margot's Study Board is full, then pass her test at the museum. Until then, she restores them for a fee.

**Can I change the difficulty?**
Yes: click the mode button beside **Begin** on the easel to go through Simplified, Relaxed, Normal and Hard. Harder modes pay more.

Everything else is on the wiki: <https://github.com/blinxxy-0/PelicanPalette/wiki/FAQ>
