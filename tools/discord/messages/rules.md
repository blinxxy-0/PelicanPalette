# Rules

**1. Be kind.** No harassment, slurs, hate or personal attacks. Respect everyone's pronouns, characters' included: Sable is they/them.

**2. Keep it safe for work.** Stardew is cosy, and so is this server. No NSFW images, links or talk, anywhere.

**3. No piracy and no reuploads.** Don't share the mod's files or art outside Nexus, and don't link pirated copies of Stardew Valley or of anyone's mods.

**4. Credit other people's work.** When you share art, a mod or a screenshot that isn't yours, say whose it is.

**5. Use the right channel.** Bugs go in {#bug-reports}, questions in {#help}, ideas in {#suggestions}. Search before you post: someone may have asked already.

**6. No spam or advertising.** Don't DM people to advertise. Mentioning your own Stardew mod in {#general} is fine when it's relevant.

**7. Hide spoilers.** Wrap heart events, the restoration test and anything from a new release in spoiler tags, ||like this||.

**8. Follow Discord's rules.** <https://discord.com/terms> and <https://discord.com/guidelines>

If something's wrong, message a **Moderator**. Moderators can remove posts and time out or remove members who break these rules.
