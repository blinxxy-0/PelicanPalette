**Translating Pelican Palette**

Thank you for helping! All the mod's text is in two files:
- `PelicanPalette/i18n/default.json`: the easel, items, skill and shop.
- `[CP] Pelican Palette/i18n/default.json`: dialogue, events, mail and the shop's lines.

Copy each one to `<language>.json` (for example `fr.json`) and translate the values. Keep the keys, anything in `{{double braces}}`, and dialogue codes like `$h` and `#$b#` as they are.

The full guide: <https://github.com/blinxxy-0/PelicanPalette/wiki/Translating>

Post your files here when they're ready (or ask about a tricky line), and say which language you're working on so nobody doubles up. Every translator is credited on the mod page.
