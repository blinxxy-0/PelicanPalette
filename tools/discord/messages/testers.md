**Testers**

Early builds are posted here before they're released. Thank you for trying them!

- Please don't share test builds, screenshots of unreleased content, or what's in them outside this channel.
- Test on a **copy** of your save, or a new farm. Test builds can break things.
- When you find a problem, start a thread on the build's post: what you did, what happened, and your SMAPI log from <https://smapi.io/log>.
