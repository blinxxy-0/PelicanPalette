# Welcome to Pelican Palette

**Pelican Palette** is a painting mod for Stardew Valley. Paint pixel art at an easel in a fishing-style minigame, level up a Painting skill, restore the valley's old paintings, and sell, gift, frame and varnish your work. An art shop opens in Cindersap Forest, run by Sable, and later Margot, a conservator from the Zuzu City museum, comes to town. You can romance either of them.

**Download:** <https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/54599>
**Wiki** (every item, recipe, price and heart event): <https://github.com/blinxxy-0/PelicanPalette/wiki>
**Needs:** SMAPI 4.0+, Content Patcher 2.9+ and SpaceCore. Generic Mod Config Menu is optional.
