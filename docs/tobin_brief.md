# Character brief: Tobin Clay, the potter (approved 2026-11-06)

1.4 and 1.5. Decided so far (2026-11-03): he lives and works in the Vale from 1.4, with dialogue, a schedule and gift tastes; his heart arc is in **1.5**, built into sculpting (the user's choice). **Pottery is its own skill in 1.5** (the user, 2026-11-07, reversing the concept sheet's "no pottery skill"): he teaches it, and his arc is told through it (`docs/design/pottery_minigame.md`).

## At a glance
- **Name:** Tobin Clay (the concept sheet's). The surname is a joke he's heard a thousand times ("It's a family trade. The name came first.").
- **Pronouns:** he/him.
- **Marriage:** no. Friendship to 10 hearts, from 1.5.
- **Age:** mid-forties.
- **Birthday:** Winter 5, when the kiln keeps the workshop the warmest house in the Vale (free in vanilla; to check against SVE).
- **Home and work:** the pottery: the workshop at the front, with the kiln, his rooms behind.
- **Role in the mod:**
  - **1.4:** sells his ceramics (decorative furniture: bowls, vases, planters, tiles) and clay; makes the Vale House's cups and the plaza's planters
  - **1.5:** teaches you Pottery at the wheel, fires your pieces in his kiln, and his arc is told through it

## Look
- **Build:** broad and solid, heavy forearms, big careful hands.
- **Hair:** close-cropped dark hair going grey; a neat dark beard.
- **Face:** deep brown skin, calm dark eyes, a face that doesn't give much away until it cracks into a laugh.
- **Clothes:** a heavy leather work apron over a rolled-up slate-grey shirt, canvas trousers, clogs.
- **Details:** dried clay slip on his forearms, always; a pencil and a wooden rib tool in his apron pocket.
- **Palette:** terracotta, slate and slip white: the workshop's colours.
- **Not like Clint or Robin:** Clint is shy and gloomy; Robin is cheerful and chatty. Tobin is calm, plain-spoken, and very dry.

## Backstory
- **The trade:** his family were potters in a Ferngill market town for four generations, making plates and jugs for farmhouses. He grew up at the wheel.
- **The Vale:** he came at thirty, when the old pottery was empty, because its kiln was the best he'd seen. He's been there since.
- **The trouble (his 1.5 arc):** people don't take his work seriously because it's useful. Visitors walk past his bowls to look at Lupini's paintings. He doesn't want fame; he wants the care in an everyday thing to be seen.
- **The arc (1.5, an outline):** you learn clay from him; he learns that what you make with your hands can be both useful and art, and that he's always known it. It ends with his work in the Exhibition Hall, on its own plinth.

## Personality and voice
- Practical, reserved and straightforward; quietly passionate about his work and dry about everything else.
- He doesn't do theory. Ask him what a pot means and he'll tell you it holds soup.
- **Speech:** short sentences, long pauses, one good joke a conversation.

Sample lines:
- First meeting: "Mind the shelf. Everything on it's for sale, and nothing's insured."
- On his work: "A painting hangs on a wall. A bowl gets used every day. Which one gets looked at more?"
- On Lupini: "We've argued about whether a bowl is art for fifteen years. I'm winning. He doesn't know yet."
- On Emmett: "I made him an ocarina. I regret it every day."

## The others
- **Lupini:** fifteen years of friendly argument about art and craft. "Land of Clay" was painted at Tobin's clay pit; he pretends not to be pleased.
- **Fern:** brings him flowers to glaze into pots; he grumbles and uses every one.
- **Bea:** the inn's cups are his.
- **Emmett:** the ocarina.
- **Clint and Robin (once the Vale's open):** he talks kilns with Clint, and Robin buys his planters.
- **Leah (1.5):** she knows wood, he knows clay. They meet over your first clay piece.

## Schedule (1.4)
- **Every day but Sunday:** the workshop, at the wheel, 9 to 5.
- **Firing days (Tuesdays and Fridays):** at the kiln; the chimney smokes, and the workshop is too hot to stay in long.
- **Sundays:** his clay pit by the river, digging; then the Vale House.
- **Evenings:** the Vale House, with Lupini, arguing.

## Gift tastes (first pass)
- **Paintings:** the mod's rule, by quality (nobody dislikes one). An **art lover** (added to `Mods/blinxxy.MuseArt/ArtLovers`), so a normal painting is liked.
- **Loves:** Clay (a joke, but he means it), Hardwood (for the kiln), a hearty meal (Spicy Eel, Survival Burger), Coffee.
- **Likes:** minerals and crystals (for glazes), cooked food, flowers (Fern's taught him).
- **Dislikes:** anything flimsy or fake: plastic, trash, gold bars ("good for nothing but looking at").

## Heart events (1.5; an outline only)
To write with the 1.5 sculpting plan. The first ideas:
- **2 hearts:** he shows you the wheel. You're bad at it; so was he.
- **4 hearts:** your first clay piece comes out of his kiln. He says one kind word about it, and goes quiet.
- **6 hearts:** a visitor buys one of his bowls to hang on the wall. He doesn't know whether to be pleased.
- **8 hearts:** Leah, Tobin and you at the clay pit: wood, clay and stone.
- **10 hearts:** his work goes into the Exhibition Hall, on its own plinth.
