## Her days
- **Fine days:** her workbench at 8; behind the counter from 11; the woods east of the Old Studios after lunch
  (fallen timber); the woods by the lake at 4 (Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays) or back to the counter (Thursdays,
  Saturdays); her bench in the evening; bed at 10.
- **Tuesdays:** the first bus to Pelican Town and Robin's shop (timber, and later joinery lessons): there by
  about 10, home by 6 (a four-hour walk each way, like the others' town days). *(Behind the town-trips switch, like the others' trips; without it, a woods day.)*
- **Sundays:** the Gilded Tree in the afternoon, studying the real tree's leaves.
- **Rain:** the studio all day: bench, counter, bench.
- **Winter:** the studio most of the day; at dusk, the top of the trail above the graveyard clearing, looking
  down at it, never going in (until her 8-heart event).
- Her counter opens whenever she's anywhere in the studio.
