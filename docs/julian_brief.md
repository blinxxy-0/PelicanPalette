# Character brief: the critic (DRAFT, for approval)

## At a glance
- **Name:** Julian Vane (decided 2026-10-31).
- **Pronouns:** he/him (decided 2026-10-31).
- **Marriage:** not marriageable. Friendship to 10 hearts.
- **Age:** about 50. He wrote Sable's review ten years ago, when Sable was in their early twenties and he was already well known.
- **Birthday:** Winter 22 (no other birthday that day), during his winter visit.
- **Home:** Zuzu City, off the map. He comes on the morning bus and leaves on the evening one.
- **Role in the mod:**
  - visits the valley for a few days each season and looks through your portfolio at the shop
  - judges the painting contest at the Stardew Valley Fair (step 5)
  - buys one painting from you each visit, at a premium
  - his heart events tell the story of the review, and end with him reviewing Sable's work again

## Look
- **Build:** tall and lean, a little stooped from reading. An angular face that looks stern until he smiles, which is rarely and usually at the wrong moment.
- **Hair:** dark, neatly combed back, silver at the temples. Clean-shaven.
- **Face:** pale skin. Thin dark rectangular glasses, which he takes off to look at a painting properly.
- **Clothes:** a long charcoal wool coat, a deep burgundy scarf, a pale grey shirt buttoned to the top.
- **Details:** a small black notebook and a silver pen, always in hand. He writes while you talk, and you never get to read it.
- **Palette:** charcoal, burgundy and silver: city colours, cold beside Sable's teal, rust and cream, and out of place in the valley.
- **Not like Elliott or Harvey:** Elliott is romantic and long-haired. Harvey is soft and anxious, with a moustache. Julian is sharp, neat and buttoned-up.

## Backstory
- **The review:** ten years ago, at a small gallery show in Zuzu City, Julian reviewed a young painter's first exhibition and called the work "technically flawless and completely empty". It was the line everyone quoted. The painter was Sable, who never showed an original again.
- **What it cost him:** the line was quoted for years, and he came to hate it. He'd written it for effect, not because it was true. He kept the show's catalogue, with a pressed note in the margin: "the third one is not empty."
- **Why he comes:** his paper wants a piece on "art in the provinces". He comes because a valley farmer's paintings turned up at a city gallery (yours, consigned through Sable), and because the shop's name in the listing is Sable's.
- **The arc:** he starts as a city snob, judging the valley's taste and yours. Through your friendship he admits the review was wrong, and that he's been afraid to say so. It ends with a second review, of Sable's new work, that says what he should have said the first time.

## Personality and voice
- Exacting, dry and funny when he means to be cruel. He enjoys a well-made sentence more than he should.
- Fair underneath. He judges the work, not the person, and he's right more often than you'd like.
- **Inside:** lonely, and sincere about art in a way he finds embarrassing. He's been hiding behind cleverness for years.
- **Speech:** long sentences with a sting at the end. He calls you "the farmer" until he respects you, then by your name.

Sample lines:
- First meeting: "Julian Vane, The Zuzu Review. No, you won't have read it. The farmer who paints, I presume. You have mud on your cuffs and cerulean on your thumb. Promising."
- On your work: "This is competent. I don't say that as an insult. Most people never get there."
- On the valley: "Everything here is so... sincere. It's exhausting. I'll be back in a season."
- Later: "I wrote one good sentence ten years ago, and it was a lie. I've been dining out on it ever since."

## Sable and Julian
- Sable recognises him at once and won't stay in the same room. Their dialogue on his visit days is shorter and colder ("He's in town. I'll be in the back.").
- They don't reconcile on screen until his last event. Even then, Sable has the last word.
- His story doesn't need Sable's romance: if you're married to Sable, the last event happens at the gallery evening; if not, at the shop.

## Visits
- **When:** three days each season (the 22nd to the 24th, clear of festivals), plus the Stardew Valley Fair in fall to judge the contest.
- **Where:** off the morning bus at 10am. He walks to the art shop, spends the afternoon around town (the museum, the Saloon, the beach), and leaves on the evening bus at 6pm.
- **Rainy days:** he stays in the Saloon all afternoon, complaining.

## Heart events (outline; scripts to be written for approval)
- **2 hearts (the shop):** he's looking through your portfolio when you come in. Sable leaves without a word. He asks who painted the third Harvest slot.
- **4 hearts (the museum):** he reviews one of your paintings aloud, honestly, and asks what you were trying to do with it. Your answer shapes his later lines.
- **6 hearts (the bus stop, evening):** he misses the bus on purpose. He tells you about the review, and shows you the note in the margin.
- **8 hearts (the Saloon):** he asks you to give Sable the old catalogue, because he can't. Sable's reaction depends on their hearts with you.
- **10 hearts (the shop, or the gallery evening if you're married to Sable):** he reviews Sable's new painting in front of them. Sable has the last word.
