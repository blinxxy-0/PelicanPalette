# Character brief: Fern Aster, the gardener (DRAFT, for approval)

1.4, the Gilded Vale. Decided so far (2026-11-03): a woman, marriageable by any farmer, one of 1.4's two romances.

## At a glance
- **Name:** Fern Aster (decided 2026-11-03; the concept sheet's Willa, renamed because it's one letter from Willy). She knows it's two plants: "My parents had one joke."
- **Pronouns:** she/her.
- **Marriage:** yes, by any farmer, like Sable and Margot.
- **Age:** late twenties.
- **Birthday:** Summer 6 (free in vanilla; to check against SVE).
- **Home:** a cottage and seedhouse beside the sculpture garden.
- **Role in the mod:**
  - tends the Vale's gardens, meadows and the overgrown sculpture garden
  - sells the Vale's wildflower seeds and odd plants from the seedhouse
  - the seasonal look of the Vale is hers

## Look
- **Build:** small, wiry, strong from digging. Never still.
- **Hair:** a long, messy sandy-blonde braid, with whatever she's been working in caught in it (a leaf, a burr, a petal).
- **Face:** tanned and freckled, a gap in her front teeth, green-brown eyes.
- **Clothes:** mustard-yellow dungarees, patched at the knees, over a faded cornflower-blue shirt; muddy boots; a battered straw sun hat with a sprig of whatever's in flower.
- **Details:** a trowel in her back pocket, seed packets in the front; dirt under her nails that never comes out.
- **Palette:** mustard, cornflower and straw: a meadow in summer. Bright beside Margot's sage and Sable's teal.
- **Not like Leah or Haley:** Leah forages and makes art from what she finds; Haley photographs flowers. Fern grows things, and lets them grow.

## Backstory
- **The Vale:** she came at twenty to apprentice with the Vale's old gardener, who was nearly ninety. When he died, the gardens were hers.
- **The trouble:** the Vale's gardens were once formal and perfect. People (guests, Lupini's buyers, a city heritage society) want them put back. She thinks that's wrong: the vines and the wildflowers are the Vale now.
- **The arc:** how much to restore, and how much to let grow. The answer she finds is not "neither" but "both, chosen carefully". It mirrors your farm: a garden you shape, and a wild edge you leave.
- **Her odd side:** she collects curious plants: a moss that only grows on old statues, a vine that flowers at night, a mushroom she swears hums. Some of them are real (in the game's forage); some are her jokes.

## Personality and voice
- Independent, patient, whimsical and stubborn.
- Kind to plants, blunt to people: she'll tell you your crops are planted too close.
- Laughs easily, loudest at herself.
- **Inside:** she's afraid that letting things grow is just an excuse for not being able to keep them.
- **Speech:** plain and warm, with a habit of talking to plants mid-conversation.

Sample lines:
- First meeting: "Mind the clover! Not you, the clover. Well. Both of you."
- On her work: "A garden isn't finished. It's just having a good year."
- On your farm: "Rows. So many rows. Don't you ever let anything just... happen?"
- Later: "My teacher said a gardener's job is to listen. I think I've only been arguing."

## The others
- **Tobin:** she brings him odd flowers to glaze into his pots. He pretends to be put out.
- **Bea:** flowers for the tea room, and a running fight about how long wildflowers last.
- **Emmett:** he writes songs about her plants. She pretends to hate it.
- **Lupini:** she's let the ivy grow over his studio window. He's never asked her to cut it.
- **Leah (in 1.5):** they meet over the sculpture garden: one wants to clear the statues, the other to keep the moss.
- **Evelyn and Caroline:** once the Vale's open, she trades cuttings with both.

## Schedule
- **Fine days:** the gardens and meadows, a different corner each day.
- **Rain:** the seedhouse, potting.
- **Mondays:** the bus to Pelican Town; Pierre's, then Evelyn's garden, then home.
- **Winter:** the seedhouse most of the day; the sculpture garden in the afternoon, clearing snow off the statues.

## Gift tastes (first pass)
- **Loves:** wildflowers (Sweet Pea, Crocus, Summer Spangle), Ancient Fruit seeds, any rare seed, Moss.
- **Likes:** flowers, forage, fertiliser ("not romantic, but useful").
- **Dislikes:** cut flowers in a bouquet ("they were alive this morning") except the Bouquet itself, which she accepts as tradition; weed killer and anything that poisons the ground.

## Heart events (outline; scripts to be written for approval)
- **2 hearts (the meadow):** she's on her knees, saving a nest of bees from the mower she refused to use. You help.
- **4 hearts (the sculpture garden):** a heritage-society letter: clear the garden or lose the grant. She shows you the moss on the oldest statue, and asks what you'd do.
- **6 hearts (your farm):** she visits for the first time, walks your fields, and is quiet. Then she asks for a corner of your farm to leave wild. A choice: yes (a small wild patch appears, with her forage), or no.
- **8 hearts (the seedhouse, at night):** the night-flowering vine blooms. She admits what she's afraid of.
- **10 hearts (the sculpture garden, dating):** she clears one path through the garden, just one, and leaves the rest. The statues on that path are the first ones you can see properly (and in 1.5, sculpt beside).
- **14 hearts (married):** a seed she planted on the farm the day she moved in has grown into something nobody's seen before.

## Marriage
- **Spouse room:** shelves of seed drawers, pressed flowers in frames, a big work table with pots.
- **Patio:** a wild flower bed that changes with the seasons.
- **Married life:** two days a week she takes the bus back to the Vale to tend its gardens; the other days she works on the farm's wild edge, and sometimes plants something without asking.
