# Character brief: Emmett Wren, the musician (DRAFT, for approval)

1.4, the Gilded Vale. Decided so far (2026-11-03): the concept sheet's Etta Wren, now a man; marriageable by any farmer, one of 1.4's two romances. His story follows the sheet's arc for Etta, extended to fourteen hearts.

## At a glance
- **Name:** Emmett Wren (decided 2026-11-03).
- **Pronouns:** he/him.
- **Marriage:** yes, by any farmer.
- **Age:** mid-thirties.
- **Birthday:** Spring 9, the week he first arrived in the Vale (free in vanilla; to check against SVE).
- **Home:** a small cottage beside the old amphitheatre.
- **Role in the mod:**
  - the Vale's music: the amphitheatre and the evenings by the fire at the Vale House
  - his songs carry the Vale's history (the artists who lived there)
  - plays at the Open House

## Look
- **Build:** lean and a little rangy; he sits on anything that isn't a chair.
- **Hair:** shaggy, sandy-brown hair that needs cutting; a short beard.
- **Face:** sun-lined from years on the road, bright blue eyes, a crooked grin.
- **Clothes:** a patched oatmeal waistcoat over a collarless shirt, a faded red neckerchief, a long travel-worn coat he won't replace. Scuffed boots.
- **Details:** a fiddle, always nearby; a little notebook of songs stuffed with scraps of paper.
- **Palette:** wren brown, oatmeal and a faded red: road colours.
- **Not like Sam or Elliott:** Sam is a young rock guitarist; Elliott is a long-haired romantic writer. Emmett is a folk fiddler, scruffy, funny, who's seen most of the Ferngill Republic from the back of a cart.

## Backstory
- **The road:** from eighteen he travelled the Republic playing fiddle at festivals, taverns, weddings and wakes, and learned the songs of every town that would teach him.
- **The Vale:** five years ago, worn out, he walked into the Vale on a spring evening and found its amphitheatre, empty and overgrown. He meant to stay a week.
- **The trouble:** he's never stayed anywhere. Part of him is still waiting to leave, and he plays for everyone and lets nobody close.
- **The arc (from the sheet):** he rediscovers the pleasure of making music with people, not performing at them. It ends with a song of his own about the Vale and the people in it. The romance adds: he learns to stay.

## Personality and voice
- Free-spirited, playful and teasing; breaks into song mid-conversation and makes up little tunes about what's happening.
- Thoughtful underneath: he's watched a lot of strangers, and notices more than he says.
- Elusive: when a conversation gets serious, he changes the subject, usually with a song.
- **Speech:** easy, rhythmic, a bit of a storyteller; he rhymes by accident and pretends it was on purpose.

Sample lines:
- First meeting: "A new face! I'll need a verse for you. What rhymes with 'farmer'? ...I'll work on it."
- On his songs: "Half of these, I'm the last person alive who knows them. That's a lot of pressure for a fiddle."
- On the Vale: "I came for a week. Five years ago. The week's going well."
- On Lupini: "He paints like he's being watched. I play like nobody's listening. One of us has it right."
- Later: "I've played in a hundred towns. I couldn't tell you which one I'm from."

## The others
- **Bea:** old friends who squabble; she hosts his evenings, he eats her pies.
- **Fern:** he writes songs about her plants; she pretends to hate them.
- **Lupini:** summer-evening walks; he's the only one who teases him.
- **Tobin:** Tobin made him a clay ocarina once. He plays it badly, on purpose, to annoy him.
- **Sam and Gus:** once the Vale's open, he sometimes plays the Saloon on a Friday. Sam's in awe; Emmett gives him fiddle lessons he doesn't want.

## Schedule
- **Mornings:** the amphitheatre, practising.
- **Afternoons:** walks the Vale; the gardens, or the riverside with his notebook.
- **Evenings:** the Vale House fire.
- **Some Fridays (after his 4-heart event):** the bus to Pelican Town, playing at the Saloon in the evening.
- **Rain:** his cottage, writing.
- **The Open House:** the amphitheatre, all afternoon.

## Gift tastes (first pass)
- **Loves:** a good meal (Pumpkin Soup, Fish Stew), Pale Ale, a Chanterelle ("tastes like autumn"), anything you made yourself.
- **Likes:** most cooked food, fruit, beer and cider, amethysts ("good for the throat, my nan said").
- **Dislikes:** expensive things that are just expensive (gold bars, diamonds) and raw ore.

## Heart events (outline; scripts to be written for approval)
- **2 hearts (the amphitheatre; the sheet's):** you find him practising alone. He tells you how he came to the Vale.
- **4 hearts (the riverside; the sheet's):** he plays you a song he's writing, and one he learned from a farmer's widow in the far north. Some of his favourite songs came from ordinary people.
- **6 hearts (the amphitheatre; the sheet's):** you help him clear the amphitheatre for a small musical evening. The residents come. It's the first time it's been used in decades. (It stays cleared.)
- **8 hearts (the Vale House, late; the sheet's ending, moved earlier):** he plays his own song about the Vale and its people, intimately, by the fire. There's a verse about you he didn't mean anyone to hear.
- **10 hearts (the overlook, dating; new):** he packs his bag, as he does every spring. Then he unpacks it, in front of you.
- **14 hearts (married; new):** he's written a song about the farm. He plays it to the animals first, because they're "a tough crowd".

## Marriage
- **Spouse room:** a corner of instruments: the fiddle, Tobin's ocarina, a concertina with a broken key; songbooks; maps of everywhere he's been, pinned to the wall.
- **Patio:** a stump where he plays at dusk (a short fiddle tune plays nearby).
- **Married life:** he plays the amphitheatre once a week, and the Saloon some Fridays. He's quietly delighted to have nowhere to go.
