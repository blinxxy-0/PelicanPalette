# Character brief: the art shop's owner (DRAFT, for approval)

## At a glance
- **Name:** Sable Okoro. They named themselves after the sable brush.
- **Alternative names:** Rowan, Wren, Ellis.
- **Pronouns:** they/them, in their own dialogue and everyone else's.
- **Marriage:** marriageable by any farmer.
- **Age:** about 30.
- **Birthday:** Fall 9 (that day has no other birthday).
- **Home and work:** the art supply shop in Cindersap Forest. They live upstairs and work the counter downstairs.
- **Role in the mod:**
  - runs the shop and the commission board
  - teaches painting
  - writes the critique line on your results screen (from 2 hearts)

## Look
- **Hair:** short, asymmetric dark hair with one stripe of teal. They say it's "an accident with a palette knife".
- **Face:** warm brown skin. Round wire glasses, usually pushed up onto their head.
- **Clothes:** a rust-coloured turtleneck with the sleeves rolled up, under an oversized canvas apron covered in years of paint.
- **Details:** a pencil behind one ear, small silver hoop earrings, and a little paint on their hands that never quite washes off.
- **Palette:** teal, rust and cream, the shop's colours. Fall-toned on purpose.
- **Not like Leah:** Leah is outdoorsy and free-spirited. Sable is precise, a city transplant, and wears an apron, not a scarf.

## Backstory
- **Before:** Sable trained and worked as a restorer at the Zuzu City Museum of Art, fixing other people's masterpieces for a living. Before that they painted their own work. A brutal gallery review in their early twenties made them stop, and they haven't painted an original since.
- **Why they came:** the museum sent them an old, damaged landscape to restore: a painting of Cindersap Forest, signed only "E.". They couldn't stop thinking about the place in it, so they quit, came to the valley, and opened a shop selling other people the tools to paint.
- **The mystery:** the painter "E." was Evelyn, decades ago. She stopped painting when she married George and never mentions it.
- **The arc:** Sable rediscovers the painter, then the painting, then themselves. It ends with them painting an original again: a portrait of you.
- **Theme:** it mirrors the minigame. You start by copying (still lifes and commissions) and end up making originals (the free canvas).

## Personality and voice
- Dry, exact and quietly funny. They notice everything: colour, light, the mud on your boots.
- Honest about your work, never cruel. Their critiques make you want to paint again, not quit.
- **Inside:** sentimental and afraid of their own taste. They hide behind "I'm just a restorer".
- **Speech:** short sentences and painter's metaphors. They answer feelings with technique until they trust you.

Sample lines:
- First meeting: "Careful, that's wet. ...Hello. You're the new farmer. You've brought half your field in on your boots. Good soil, though."
  - (Changed 2026-10-25 from "a lot of brown on you": the farmer's colouring is the player's choice, and the line could read as being about skin.)
- Rainy day: "Rain's the best light there is. Flat and honest. Nothing to hide behind."
- Critique (results screen): "Your greens went muddy. Mud is just green that got distracted."
- Critique, high praise: "...I'd hang that. I don't say that."
- Loved gift: "Oh. Where did you find this? I'm keeping it somewhere the light can't fade it."
- Hated gift: "No. Absolutely not. Take it back before it touches anything."
- 8 hearts and up: "I used to think I fixed paintings so I'd never have to finish one. You're ruining that for me."

## Relationships in town
- **Leah:** they respect each other and compete a little. They trade supplies, and argue about whether sculpture "counts as colour".
- **Evelyn:** the mystery. Kindly, oblivious at first; their 8-heart event resolves it.
- **Elliott:** bookish friends. Sable paints his book covers, and he narrates their bad days back to them.
- **Emily:** they trade dyes and pigments and talk about colour all night.
- **Haley:** an ongoing friendly debate about photography versus painting, which eventually turns into mutual respect.
- **Robin:** built their counter and easels, and is always trying to sell them shelving.
- **Marnie and Linus:** neighbours. Linus brings them berries and flowers for pigment.

## Schedule (sketch)
- **Mon, Tue, Thu, Sat:**
  - 8:00 plein-air painting by the Forest pond (sunny days)
  - 10:00–18:00 at the counter
  - evenings in the shop's back room
- **Wed:** shop closed. Walks to town for the library and museum, and visits Evelyn (later events build on this).
- **Fri:** counter until 17:00, then the Saloon with Elliott and Leah.
- **Sun:** paints at the beach in the morning, at the counter in the afternoon.
- **Rain:** paints inside all day, at the counter 10:00–18:00.
- **Festivals:** a placement and dialogue at every festival. At the Fair they run a small paint-your-own-sign booth. That's flavour text only: no minigame, it's just set dressing.

## Heart events (outline)
- **Intro:** a "Grand opening" letter on spring 2 of year 1. The first visit plays a short scene: they're hanging the palette sign, and they give you 3 canvases and a jar of Rose.
- **2 hearts (the shop):** you catch them restoring a damaged forest landscape. "It's why I'm here. Don't laugh."
- **4 hearts (the pond, sunny morning):** you paint side by side. They teach you a mixing trick, which rewards a Sable brush. You notice they only ever sketch, never finish.
- **6 hearts (the shop, evening):** a letter from Zuzu City offers them their old job back. They admit they haven't painted their own work in ten years. Your answer shapes the dialogue: "stay", "it's your call" or "paint something first".
- **8 hearts (Evelyn's house):** Sable brings the restored landscape, and Evelyn recognises it. She painted it as a girl, before the farm and before George. Evelyn and Sable paint together.
- **10 hearts (the shop, after hours):** they unveil their first original in years: a portrait of you. It's a special framed painting you can hang. (Bouquet hand-off comes before this.)
- **14 hearts (married):** a small exhibition of their originals at the farmhouse, with townspeople visiting. Evelyn's landscape hangs at the centre.

## Marriage
- **Spouse room:** a studio corner with an easel holding a half-finished canvas, a paint-splattered rug, stacked frames and a window.
- **Spouse patio:** an outdoor easel facing the crops.
- **Married life:** sometimes leaves a pigment jar or a canvas on the table in the morning. On rainy days they paint in the house. They keep running the shop: they commute there in the mornings.
