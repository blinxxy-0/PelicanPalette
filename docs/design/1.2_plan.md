# Pelican Palette 1.2: the portfolio, the record, and the festivals

Planning notes, 2026-10-31. Nothing here is built yet.

**The goal:** move from a painting mod with one NPC toward an expansion, where painting reaches across the whole game. 1.2 does that through four things:
- a Community Center-style **portfolio** to fill
- a reorganised **painting record**
- new **places and subjects** that open up as you progress
- the **festivals**

The critic is the new character who ties them together.

**Decided so far (2026-10-31):**
- **No pigments locked behind progression.** Players on Normal and Hard buy or craft their colours, and early, easy subjects must never become unpaintable.
- **The portfolio is our own system, not Unlockable Bundles.** Unlockable Bundles uses up what you give it and matches only on context tags. All paintings are one item, so a slot couldn't tell a Summit view from a parsnip, and it couldn't show the actual painting.
- **The portfolio must not undercut Sable's gallery evening.** The evening still celebrates Sable's work, and your portfolio has a part in it (section 2).
- **Saved for 1.3:**
  - special orders
  - a Painting Mastery
  - a second romanceable NPC

---

## 1. The back room

A new small room behind the shop, through a door beside the counter. Sable keeps their stock there and lets you keep your work there too. It holds two things:

- **The portfolio book**, on a lectern: the Community Center part (below).
- **The plan chest**: wide, flat drawers for any other paintings you want to keep. There's no limit and no conditions. It's storage, so kept paintings stop crowding your chests. You can take paintings out any time.

**Frames aren't needed.** Artists keep their portfolios unframed, and framing is for hanging. Because any painting can go in, there's no wrong item to hand in and nothing for players to report as a bug. A framed painting is accepted and comes back out framed.

**Opening times:** the back room opens with the shop, and also any time you're married to Sable. Same rule as the counter.

**Building it:** a new map, made the same way as the shop (`tools/build_shop_interior.py`), with its own door. The shop's map is already full, so the shop gets a doorway, not a new corner. Sable's room downstairs stays as it is.

## 2. The portfolio book

**How it works:**
- Laid out like the Community Center's bundle rooms. Each **page** has a theme and 4 to 6 **slots**.
- **One book for the whole farm**, like the Community Center. In multiplayer anyone can fill a slot, and each painting shows who painted it.
- Each slot asks for a particular painting, checked against what's painted, its season and its quality.

**Your painting fills the slot:**
- The slot shows **your painting**, drawn from its saved pixels, never a generic icon.
- Paintings in the book stay there. They're kept, not used up.
- You can **swap in a better painting** later, and the old one comes back to you. A filled slot never goes empty again, so a page you've finished stays finished.

**Rewards:**
- Finishing a page gives a reward and a short note from Sable.
- Finishing the whole book gives a larger reward (ideas below).

### Pages

Early, easy pages sit beside late-game ones, as in the Community Center. Every slot names exactly what it wants, and a hint shows when a slot can't be filled yet (for example "Fix the bus to reach the desert").

| Page | Slots | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| **The Four Seasons** | A view of Pelican Town in each season, and one of your farm. | Year 1 |
| **Harvest** | A still life of a crop from each season, and one of three crops together. | Year 1 |
| **Far Places** | The Calico Desert, the Skull Cavern entrance, Ginger Island, the Secret Woods, the Quarry. | Each as you reach it |
| **Legends** | Crimsonfish, Angler, Legend, Glacierfish, Mutant Carp. | Each once caught (they're still lifes, painted from the fish in your bag) |
| **Faces of the Valley** | Portraits, in groups: the Saloon regulars, a family, Sable. | 4 hearts with each sitter (the current portrait rule) |
| **Fine Work** | A gold painting, an iridium one, a glazed one, a varnished one, a Masterpiece. | Painting levels 4 to 8 |

**The Summit only opens at Perfection**, so it can't be on a page. Instead it closes the book (decided 2026-10-31):
- Once the book is finished, painting the view from the Summit plays a short closing scene.
- Sable joins you at the top and looks through your finished portfolio. If you're married, it's tender; if not, it's warm and proud.
- If the critic's story is finished, the critic is mentioned.
- The game walks the scene and you don't take control (your rule for cutscenes).
- The Summit view then goes into the book as its last page.

**The book's final reward (decided 2026-10-31): a gold-leaf frame.**
- A one-off Picture Frame, finished in gold leaf, from Sable with a letter.
- A painting in it is worth more and shows a gold frame when hung.
- Exact value to be set by the economy check.
- Then the Summit scene (above) closes the book.

## 3. The gallery evening (Sable's 14-heart event)

The evening still belongs to Sable. Your portfolio appears beside their work, not instead of it.

**During the event:**
- The portfolio book lies open on a table among the panels.
- One villager pages through it and names an actual painting of yours: a portrait of themselves if one is there, otherwise your best painting. For example: "Is this my portrait? Since when do I look that serious?"
- A custom event command picks the painting and fills in the line, much as `blinxxy.MuseArt_GiveFarmerPortrait` does now.
- The player doesn't take control during the event (your rule), so the book is something characters react to, not something you click.

**After the event:** the book is displayed on a stand in the shop, where anyone can look through it.

**If the book is empty:** Sable has put out a small pile of your sketches instead, and the line changes to suit.

## 4. The painting record, reorganised

The record now lives at the counter as one long list. 1.2 turns it into a book with tabs:

- **Tabs:** Crops, Forage, Fish, Minerals, Cooking, Portraits, Views, and Other.
- **Silhouettes:** things you haven't painted yet show as silhouettes, like the Collections tab. Hovering one tells you how to unlock it.
- **Your painting as the picture:** when the portfolio or plan chest holds a painting of a subject, the record shows that painting, not the item's icon. This is what ties the record to the portfolio.
- **Tab rewards:** each tab gives small rewards at 25%, 50% and 100%. These sit alongside the current milestones (5, 15, 30, 50 different subjects), which stay as they are.
- **Saves:** the record's data doesn't change. Only the menu is new (`Menus/CollectionMenu.cs`), so old saves just work.

## 5. New places and subjects

**Plein air views** need an easel set up outdoors (`Views.Take` checks `IsOutdoors`). Most far places are outdoors already, so they work with no changes:
- the Desert
- Ginger Island
- the Secret Woods
- the Quarry
- the Summit

**No views indoors (decided 2026-10-31).** Far Places uses the Skull Cavern entrance, which is outdoors in the desert, not the bottom of the Mines, so plein air stays outdoors only and needs no changes. (Still to check: whether the Volcano's summit, the Caldera, counts as outdoors. If it does, it could be a bonus slot.)

**New subjects:**
- **Monsters**, once slain (the Adventurer's Guild count). The reference is the monster's own sprite, the way portraits use a villager's.
- **Junimos**, once the Community Center or Joja route is finished.
- **Krobus**, and the **Wizard**, after their quests. These are portraits that don't need hearts.
- **Legendary fish** already work as still lifes, and only need a slot in the book.

## 6. The critic

Sable told you about a critic in their 6-heart event, who called their work "technically flawless and completely empty". **Decided (2026-10-31): the new character is that same critic, Julian Vane.** Julian (he/him) arriving in the valley gives him a story built in.

**Who they are:**
- Not romanceable.
- Clever, sharp-tongued, and fairer than Sable remembers.
- The **judge** at the Fair contest (section 7).
- Visits the valley once a season for a few days, staying at the Saloon or at Haley and Emily's house.
- Doesn't need Sable's romance to make sense.

**What they do while visiting:**
- Look through your portfolio at the shop and give an honest review of it.
- Give you advice that improves with your Painting level.
- Buy one painting of yours a season at a premium (an outlet for Masterpieces).

**Their story, told over heart events with them:**
- Sable sees them and won't stay in the same room.
- You learn the review cost the critic something too.
- At the end, the critic reviews your work and then Sable's again. If you're married to Sable, it happens at the gallery evening. If not, it happens at the shop.

**Art:** portraits and sprites matching the base game in palette, detail, sizes and frames, the same as Sable. Suggested look for Julian, to confirm when art starts: sharp and expensive, out of place in the valley. A long dark coat, a scarf, glasses, and a notebook always in hand.

## 7. Festivals

**Stardew Valley Fair: the painting contest**
- A table beside the grange display, run by the critic.
- You enter one painting from your bag. It's judged against three rivals (Leah, Evelyn after her story, and one more) on stars, quality, how hard the subject was, and its finish.
- The result is announced when Lewis judges the grange, so it keeps to the Fair's own rhythm.
- **Prizes:** star tokens (1st 1000, 2nd 500, 3rd 200), and a ribbon you can hang in the shop or at home. Winning also unlocks a line of dialogue from the critic.
- **Note:** the base game judges the grange in its own code, so our contest runs alongside it and doesn't replace anything. Other mods that change the Fair's map (Stardew Valley Expanded, for example) need checking.

**The contest's mechanics (proposed 2026-10-31, for approval):**
- **Where:** Julian stands at a small table with a display easel on the open cobblestone left of the grange display (around x 31, y 57 on the Fair's map).
- **Entering:** talk to Julian before Lewis judges, and pick one painting from your bag. He keeps it on the easel, so it shows at the Fair, and gives it back after judging. One entry per player.
- **Judging:** happens when Lewis judges the grange, out of 100:
  - quality: 30 normal, 45 silver, 60 gold, 75 iridium
  - stars: up to 15 (3 a star)
  - subject difficulty: up to 5
  - finish: satin +3, gloss +5, gallery +8
  - a Masterpiece: +10
- **The rivals:** Leah (60 to 78), Elliott (52 to 70), and Emily (45 to 65), with Evelyn replacing Elliott once she's painting again after Sable's 8-heart event. Each Fair rolls their scores fresh.
- **Prizes:** star tokens like the grange's: 1st 1,000, 2nd 500, 3rd 200, 4th 50. Placing also brings a ribbon to hang, in blue, red or yellow.
- **The result:** talk to Julian after judging. He says where you placed, in a line that suits it, and hands the painting back.

**Night Market: Sable's boat stall**
- Sable sets up on one of the market's boats, all three nights.
- They buy unusual paintings at 1.5x: night or rain views, Ginger Island, legendary fish and monsters.
- They sell one thing you can't get elsewhere, either a moonlight frame or a tube of a colour only sold at night (to be decided). Since it's a sale, not a lock, it doesn't break the pigment rule.

**The stall's mechanics (proposed 2026-10-31, for approval):**
- **Where:** the market's five boats all belong to its vendors, so Sable brings a small painted skiff of their own. It's moored below the submarine's dock on the west pier (around x 4 to 10, y 37 to 41), and you talk to them from the dock. Two of the floating lanterns there move aside.
- **How it's built:** the way the game builds its own vendors (Lupini, the merchants). The boat and Sable standing in it are tiles added to the market's map, with an action on Sable that opens the stall. Art: a PixelLab skiff, with Sable's own standing sprite.
- **When:** all three nights, from 5pm, when the market opens. On those evenings Sable leaves the shop at 5pm and isn't seen in town.
- **What they sell:** the **Moonlit Frame**, a Picture Frame finished in silver and midnight blue. A painting framed in it is worth 25% more and hangs in the moonlit frame. One a night, 1,500g. It's built as a frame style, which the gold-leaf frame (the portfolio's final reward) can then reuse.
- **What they buy, at 1.5x:** unusual paintings. That means views of the Far Places (desert, Skull Cavern, Ginger Island, Secret Woods), the legendary fish, monsters, and night views. Paintings don't remember when they were painted yet, so from 1.2 a view painted after 7pm or in the rain is marked as a night (or rain) view. Up to three a night.
- **Lupini:** Lupini, the market's painter, is three boats along. Sable has a line about them ("The only other painter in the valley who works at night.").

**Possible additions, if they fit:**
- **Spirit's Eve:** one spooky subject.
- **Feast of the Winter Star:** Sable gives one of your paintings as their secret-friend gift.

## 8. Build order

1. **The record, reorganised.** The categories it introduces are used by everything else. *Built 2026-10-31* (`Painting/RecordTabs.cs`, `Menus/CollectionMenu.cs`; tested with `museart_recordtest`). Your own painting as a tile's picture waits for the portfolio (step 2).
2. **The back room, the plan chest and the portfolio book**, with pages 1, 2 and 6 (Four Seasons, Harvest, Fine Work), which need nothing new. *Built 2026-10-31* (`Painting/Portfolio.cs`, `Menus/PortfolioMenu.cs`, `Shop/PlanChest.cs`, `tools/build_back_room.py`; tested with `museart_portfoliotest`). The plan chest holds 70 (a big chest), not unlimited. Sable's trapdoor moved into the back room at the user's request.
3. **New places and subjects**, then the Far Places and Legends pages. *Built 2026-10-31* (`Painting/Creatures.cs`; tested with `museart_creaturetest`). Changes from the plan: creatures are picked from a slot on the easel's setup screen and have their own record tab; the Quarry is part of the Mountain's map and the Skull Cavern's mouth part of the desert's, so Far Places uses the Skull Cavern's entry room (allowed as an indoor view, being a lasting place) and Ginger Island's north instead.
4. **The critic:** data, art, schedule, then their events. *Built 2026-10-31* (`Social/Julian.cs`, `Social/JulianEvents.cs`, `data/julian*.json`, `tools/build_julian_events.py`; scripts approved in `docs/julian_events.md`; tested with `museart_juliantest`, `museart_eventpaths` and `museart_dialoguefit`).
5. **The Fair contest**, which needs the critic. *Built 2026-10-31* (`Social/PaintingContest.cs`; ribbons in `tools/art/julian_items.py`; tested with `museart_contesttest`). Julian's table moved at the user's request from beside the strength game to the grass just east of the grange row (48, 59); his easel is a festival prop, so it's solid.
6. **The Night Market stall.** *Built 2026-10-31* (`Social/NightMarketStall.cs`, `tools/build_night_market_stall.py`, `tools/art/moonlit_frame.py`; the skiff is PixelLab's skiff_gallery_2, the user's pick; tested with `museart_stalltest`). Changes from the plan: the skiff moors at x 4 to 9, y 37 to 41, right under the bridge (the user found it too low); the mod draws Sable aboard only from 5pm, so they're not on the boat while still in the shop; and the Moonlit Frame is a look only, since a framed painting has no sale value for "25% more" to apply to (nor will the gold-leaf frame, a question for the economy check).
7. **The gallery evening's portfolio scene**, which needs the book.
8. **Testing and finishing:**
   - multiplayer
   - compatibility with Stardew Valley Expanded and Polyamory Sweet
   - the wiki and the Nexus page

Each step gets tests in the dev game (`DevGame.cs`) and screenshots checked at both UI sizes before it's called done.
