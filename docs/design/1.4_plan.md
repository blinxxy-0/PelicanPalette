# Pelican Palette 1.4: the Gilded Vale, Lupini, and the chisel

Planning notes, 2026-11-03. A draft for review: nothing here is built.

**Decided so far:**
- **Two releases (decided 2026-11-03):** 1.4 is the Gilded Vale (the bus, the map, the five residents, Lupini, Emmett, the Open House). 1.5 is Sculpting (the skill, Leah's events, Tobin through clay, monuments, painted sculpture). The sculpting mechanics pass can start during 1.4. Sections 4 and 5 stay here for now and move to a 1.5 plan when it's written.
- **Lupini (decided 2026-11-03):** a friend to ten hearts, not a romance; he/him. Section 3's five events.
- **Names (decided 2026-11-03):** the innkeeper is **Bea Thistle** (the sheet's Mara Bell: too close to Margot Pell, then Mara Thistle: one letter from Maru). The gardener is **Fern Aster** (the sheet's Willa Aster: one letter from Willy).
- **Hearts and romance (decided 2026-11-03):**
  - **Tobin's arc is in 1.5**, built into sculpting. In 1.4 he lives and works in the Vale with dialogue, a schedule and gifts.
  - **Everyone else gets full friendship hearts**, ten hearts with events like any vanilla villager: Lupini, Bea, Fern, Emmett.
  - **Two of them are marriageable: one man, one woman.** None of the residents but Lupini and Tobin is a man, so one of the women becomes a man. That makes four marriage candidates in the mod: Sable, Margot and these two.
  - **Which two (decided 2026-11-03): Fern Aster, and Etta as a man, Emmett Wren** (he/him). Bea stays a woman and a friend. Section 2.

**The goal:** 1.3 gave painting an endgame. 1.4 opens a world beyond the valley and a second art:
- **the Gilded Vale**, a small artists' settlement reached by bus, with its own five residents
- **Lupini**, the Night Market's painter, as a full character who lives there, with heart events
- **Sculpting**, a second skill and minigame, with Leah at the heart of its story
- two of Claude's own ideas (section 5)

The Vale comes from the user's concept sheet, `Gilded_Vale_Design_Notes.md` (ChatGPT and Teams, 2026-11-03). Its history, cast, layout, Lupini's arc and Open House are kept as written unless a change is noted here. Its map image (`Gilded_Vale_Map_Concept.png`) wasn't in Downloads with the notes.

**What ties the three together:** the Vale is where the valley's artists once went, so it's where Lupini lives, and its overgrown **sculpture garden** is where sculpting's story leads.

## 2. The Gilded Vale

### Getting there

Pam's bus gets a second destination. At the ticket machine you choose the Desert or the Vale.
- **When it opens:** a letter after the Night Market, once you've bought one of Lupini's paintings or have Painting 3 (to decide), and once the bus is repaired, as for the Desert.
- **Compatibility:** Pathoschild's **Central Station** (1.6) is how content packs share bus, boat and train destinations. Register the Vale there when it's installed; use our own ticket-machine choice when it isn't. Check how Stardew Valley Expanded handles the bus.
- **Coming back:** the bus stop in the Vale, the same way.

### The map

As the sheet lays it out: the bus stop and arrival sign to the north; homes and studios above; the plaza and Exhibition Hall in the centre; the amphitheatre, sculpture garden, riverside walk and overlook to the south. Compact: about the size of the Desert plus the Oasis's interior, not a second Pelican Town.
- **Terraced, with a river** coming down to a small lake by the overlook (decided 2026-11-03). Terraces give the Vale a shape of its own (Pelican Town is flat), and the river gives Fern's gardens and the riverside walk something to follow.
- **Interiors:** the Vale House (with Bea's rooms upstairs), Lupini's atelier, Fern's seedhouse, Tobin's workshop, Emmett's cottage, the Exhibition Hall. Six, or seven with one of the Old Studios (proposed in the map's design).
- **The map's design (draft, 2026-11-03):** `docs/design/gilded_vale_map.md`, with the sketch `art/gilded_vale/layout_v1.png`. The layout is as the sheet has it, except Fern's cottage, which sits by the sculpture garden on the middle terrace.
- **Views to paint:** the overlook, the plaza, the amphitheatre, the sculpture garden, the lake. They go into the painting record. **Not a seventh page of the portfolio:** finishing every page gives the gold-leaf frame and the Summit scene (1.3), so a new page would un-finish players' completed books. If the Vale gets a page, it's a bonus page outside the count, or a sketchbook of its own (see section 9).
- **The rule for art:** like the 1.3 maps: vanilla palette, buildings front-facing with big roofs, props inside their plots, nothing straddling walls.

## 4. Sculpting

A second skill, built like Painting: a SpaceCore skill from 1 to 10 with six professions, a minigame, tools with wear, recipes gated by level, and finished pieces that sell, gift and decorate.

### The minigame

**It must feel different from painting.** Painting is about colour, filling cells and light. Sculpting is about **taking away**: you can't put stone back.

**The core idea: two views.** You carve the block from the **front**, then turn it and carve from the **side**. The sculpture is what's left of both cuts, so the front and the side together make a shape neither could alone. The reference shows both. That's what makes it "more involved" than painting without making it busier.

**Its stages, one at a time** (the rule for minigames):
1. **Roughing out.** A big chisel takes off large pieces. Each strike has a **force meter**, like casting a rod: too soft does nothing, too hard cracks the block. Material has **grain**: strikes along it are safe, across it risky.
2. **Detail.** Smaller tools, cell by cell, matching the reference's silhouette. What runs out is **the block's patience**: mistakes add stress, and too much cracks the piece. No light meter.
3. **Finishing.** Sanding or polishing: a short rhythm stage that sets the surface (rough, smooth, polished) and the stars.

**Hard** runs stress, grain and a time limit together, as painting's Hard does.

**Quality:** how well the shape matches the reference from both views (like the painting judge's stars), plus skill.

### Materials

| Material | From | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **Wood** | Hardwood, driftwood | Leah's. Forgiving, strong grain. The first. |
| **Stone** | The Quarry; Clint | Harder, cracks if forced. Mid-game. |
| **Marble** | Rare: the Quarry, Skull Cavern | The best results, the least forgiving. |
| **Ice** | Winter only, from the farm pond | Melts: a timed, seasonal challenge. Maybe the Festival of Ice. |

**Clay is Pottery's now** (the user, 2026-11-07): pottery is its own skill, with the wheel, banding and stoking, in 1.5 beside Sculpting. Tobin teaches it, and his arc (craft vs. fine art) is told through it. See `docs/design/pottery_minigame.md`.

### What you can sculpt

Furniture first: **statues, busts, garden figures, bowls and vases, reliefs** to hang on walls. Subjects come from where painting's do: villagers (busts, at 4 hearts), animals, creatures, fish.

### Leah's role

Leah is canonically a sculptor who left Zuzu City to make art. She's **the teacher**:
- Her first sculpting event gives you a chisel and teaches you wood.
- She knows of the Vale: she went once, years ago, and never went back (why is her story). She takes you to its **sculpture garden**, and the old sculptures there are what she wants to learn from.
- Her canon events (the 8-heart art show, her marriage) are untouched. Ours are **friendship** events, so they work whether or not you marry her, with extra lines if you have.

## 5. Claude's two ideas

**A. Monuments.** Sculpture's big commissions, placed **for good**: a statue in Pelican Town's square, a figure on the bridge to the beach, and in the Vale, the empty plinths in the sculpture garden (the sheet's "player contributions"). Each is a large piece and a material cost, asked by Lewis or by the Vale's residents. A reward you see every day; a handful of them, as the sheet asks, not a customisable map.

**B. Painted sculpture.** The bridge between the two skills: a finished sculpture can be **painted** at the easel (the painting minigame, on the sculpture's shape). It's worth more and needs both skills. Sable and Leah's joint event brings it in: the valley's two artists, finally working together.

## 9. Consistency with 1.2 and 1.3 (checked 2026-11-04)

Every brief and plan item was checked against the mod as it is (the briefs, events, wiki and code). What changed, and the rules for building 1.4:

- **Julian:** his canon is one cruel, famous sentence about Sable ten years ago ("dining out on it ever since"). Lupini's making review is an earlier rave he barely remembers, twenty years ago; Lupini is about fifty, Julian's age. Julian's visits stay the 22nd to 24th of each season plus the Fair; the **Open House (Fall 7) is an extra visit**, off the morning bus like the others.
- **Margot:** her Tuesday and Thursday afternoons in the Painting Wing stay. Fall 7 is a Sunday, her free day, so the Open House fits.
- **Exhibitions, three kinds, kept apart:**
  - Sable's 14-heart exhibition (1.2) is Sable showing their own originals at the shop's gallery.
  - The museum's Painting Wing (1.3) holds the restored old paintings.
  - The Vale's Exhibition Hall holds the Vale's own artists, past and present, and your contributions, and the Vale's restored finds.
  - Lupini's 8-heart event reopens the Hall for everyone else's work, not his own.
- **The portfolio** stays complete at its current pages (the gold-leaf frame, the Summit scene). No new counted page.
- **The museum collection** stays at 21. The Vale's damaged finds are their own, shown in the Hall.
- **Gifts:** paintings follow the mod's rule (by quality; nobody dislikes one). **Lupini, Tobin, Emmett and Fern join the art lovers** (`ArtItems.DefaultArtLovers`, and the wiki's Gifting Paintings page). Bea doesn't.
- **Easels:** Lupini's 10-heart easel is furniture only; Painting Mastery's master's easel stays the only one with a bonus. A Lupini frame, if chosen instead, goes into the frames table with a rarity and trait like the others.
- **Commissions:** if Lupini or the Vale ask for paintings, it's through the existing commission board or special orders, not a new system.
- **Schedules:** Bea shops in town on Thursdays (Pierre's closes on Wednesdays). Emmett at the Saloon on some Fridays fits the 1.3 Saloon scenes (Julian and Margot's).
- **Themes:** Sable (stopped after failure), Margot (never made her own), Lupini (lost the joy after success) are all about making art. Emmett's arc is now about belonging, not performance, so it doesn't repeat them. Tobin (craft as art, 1.5), Bea (what she wants for herself), Fern (preserve or let grow) are different already.
- **Staying the night at the Vale House (decided 2026-11-04): a real overnight stay, like Ginger Island.** It uses the game's own sleep: a `Bed` and `TouchAction Sleep` bed, and `AllowWakeUpWithoutBed T` on the map, so you wake at the inn. Only paying Bea for the room is new code (`docs/bea_brief.md`).
- **Fern's wild corner of the farm: dropped (2026-11-04).** Her 6-heart event gives you a packet of the season's wild seeds instead, so nothing changes the farm itself.
- **Birthdays:** none clash with vanilla or the mod's (Sable Fall 9, Margot Spring 19, Julian Winter 22). Lupini's Winter 18 is the day after the Night Market, where Sable's stall stays as it is. Checked against SVE (2026-11-08): only Fern clashed (Summer 6, Martin), so she moved to Summer 15.
