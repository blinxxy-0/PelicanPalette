# Character brief: Opal and Jet Corrie, the glassworkers (approved 2026-11-08)

1.4, the Gilded Vale. Decided so far (the user, 2026-11-08): twins in their sixties, stained glass and
mosaic, opposites who need each other. They grew up in the Vale's Studio II at their mother Mabel's bench,
kept the studio after her, then left for Zuzu City and built a successful glass company. They sold it and
come home to buy back the studio they were raised in. Friends to ten hearts, not marriageable. The sale and
renovation around them: `docs/design/vale_newcomers.md`.

Lines marked *(Claude's call)* are Claude's drafting, open to change.

## At a glance
- **Names:** **Opal** (sister) and **Jet** (brother) Corrie *(the user)*. Mabel named them after her glass:
  opal glass is milky and glows, jet is black and sharp. They've heard every joke about it.
- **Pronouns:** Opal she/her, Jet he/him.
- **Marriage:** no. Each is a friend to ten hearts, with their own hearts, gifts and events
  *(Claude's call: two NPCs, not one shared meter)*.
- **Age:** about sixty *(Claude's guess, accepted in playback)*. Ten years older than Lupini.
- **Birthday:** **Summer 2**, both of them *(Claude's call: free in vanilla and the mod, no festival; to
  check against SVE)*. The start of the long summer light, for glass.
- **Home and work:** Studio II of the Old Studios: their workshop at the front, a bed each at either end.
- **Arrival:** Spring 1, Year 3, after the For Sale notice (Fall, Year 2), the Sold notice (Winter) and
  Robin's renovation *(the user)*.
- **Role in the mod:**
  - sell stained-glass furniture at their counter: new windows, lamps and mosaic tiles beside a selection
    of the game's own glass and lamp pieces *(the user)*
  - restore the Exhibition Hall's old windows once the Hall reopens (Lupini's 8-heart event)
  - the Vale's living memory of its full days, from the inside: they grew up in it

## Look *(Claude's call)*
- **Opal:** short and round, a cloud of silver curls held up with a pencil; jewel-coloured scarves (ruby,
  cobalt) and rings of her own glass on every finger; reading glasses on a beaded chain; city clothes,
  well cut, that she gets solder on anyway.
- **Jet:** tall and thin, close-cropped grey hair, clean-shaven; a black roll-neck under a leather apron;
  safety glasses pushed up on his head; a small scar across one knuckle from a cut sixty years ago.
- **Palette:** Opal is the colours glass throws on the floor (ruby, cobalt, amber); Jet is the lead between
  them (black, slate, pewter). Side by side they look like a window.
- **Not like anyone else:** the valley's only old artists who aren't retired; nothing like Evelyn and
  George, or Lupini's tired fame.

## Backstory
- **Mabel Corrie** worked stained glass in Studio II for 24 summers. The twins grew up at her bench,
  sweeping offcuts, then cutting them. When she died, they kept the studio eight more summers. She's buried
  in the Vale's graveyard under a stone Bram Fairlie carved, with a glazed urn at its foot (the graveyard
  design); her painted echo in the Gilded Grove holds a piece of glass to the light. Visiting her grave is
  part of coming home, and the twins can talk about it.
- **Lupini** arrived in their time, about thirty years ago, a young painter in Studio I. They knew him
  before he was famous.
- **Leaving:** about twenty years ago, in their late thirties, they left for Zuzu City. Opal wanted more
  than the Vale could give; Jet went because Opal went *(Claude's call)*.
- **Corrie Glass:** they built it into a successful company: station roofs, hotel lobbies, church windows
  across the region. Opal ran the business, Jet ran the workshop, and over the years both did less making
  and more managing.
- **Coming home *(the user)*:** a bigger firm bought Corrie Glass. They found they hated managing instead
  of making, cashed out, and when their old studio came up for sale they bought it before anyone else
  could.
- **The arc *(Claude's call)*:** whether they can still make things with their own hands after twenty
  years of running a company, and whether they can do it together, on equal terms, without the company
  between them.

## Personality and voice
- **Opal:** loud, warm, a born haggler; draws the designs and talks to everyone; laughs before the joke's
  finished. **Inside:** she's afraid her hands have forgotten, and that coming home was running away from
  being sold off.
- **Jet:** quiet and exact; cuts the glass, leads the joins, sets the mosaic pieces; says one sentence where
  Opal says ten, and it's usually the right one. **Inside:** he never wanted to leave, and has never said so.
- **Together:** every window is an argument they both enjoy *(the user)*. They correct each other's stories
  mid-sentence.

Sample lines *(Claude's call)*:
- Opal, first meeting: "You're the farmer! Of course you are. You've got the hands. Come in, mind the
  glass, everything sharp is Jet's."
- Jet, first meeting: "Hm. Don't touch the blue."
- Opal, on the company: "Twenty years of meetings about glass. Not one of them was made of glass."
- Jet, on Opal: "She designs it. I make it possible. She'd tell you it's the other way round."
- Opal, on Lupini: "He had one coat and no money and he painted the lake purple. We thought he was mad. We
  were right, and look at him now."

## The others *(Claude's call)*
- **Lupini:** old friends from before he was famous. Opal teases him about his first paintings; Jet
  quietly kept one. The Hall's windows bring the three of them together.
- **Bea:** they remember her great-aunt running the inn, and Bea as a little girl visiting in summer.
  Bea finds it unsettling and lovely in equal parts.
- **Tobin:** two kilns in a small valley. A friendly rivalry about temperature, and Tobin's pots on their
  windowsills.
- **Fern:** Jet mends the cracked panes of her glass seedhouse at dawn, unasked, and says nothing about it.
- **Emmett:** one of his old songs about the Vale has a verse about a glassworker. Opal knows it's Mabel.
- **Tess:** they grew up next door to Bram Fairlie in Studio III, and were in the Vale when he died. Jet recognises Bram's tools in Tess's
  hands before anyone, and keeps her secret until she's ready (`docs/tess_brief.md`).
- **Margot:** she knows Corrie Glass by reputation and doesn't approve of its methods (too much
  replacement, not enough conservation). Opal enjoys the argument; Jet quietly agrees with Margot.
- **Robin:** she renovated their studio, and Opal still hasn't stopped telling her how good a job it was.

## Schedule *(Claude's call)*
- **Weekdays:** the studio, counter open mornings; Opal out in the afternoons (the plaza, the Vale House),
  Jet in the workshop.
- **Once the Hall has reopened:** two days a week both at the Hall, working on its windows.
- **Early mornings:** Jet walks to Fern's seedhouse.
- **Rain:** both in the workshop all day; the coloured light on the floor goes grey.
- **Saturdays:** Opal takes the bus to Pelican Town (Pierre's, the Saloon in the evening); Jet stays.
- **Winter:** the kiln keeps them in; Opal visits Tobin to argue about whose workshop is warmer.

## Gift tastes (first pass) *(Claude's call)*
- **Paintings:** the mod's rule, by quality. Both are **art lovers**.
- **Both love:** Refined Quartz (glass is made of it), Fire Quartz, Prismatic Shard.
- **Opal also loves:** Fairy Rose, Pink Cake. **Jet also loves:** Triple Shot Espresso, Jade.
- **Likes:** gems and minerals, flowers (Opal), cooked fish (Jet).
- **Dislikes:** anything mass-made in the city's style (Joja Cola for both); Jet dislikes anything sticky.

## Heart events (outline; scripts to be written for approval) *(Claude's call)*

Each twin has five events. Both twins appear in most of them; whose hearts trigger it decides whose story
it is.

**Opal**
- **2 hearts (the studio):** she's unpacking a crate of city lamps, each more expensive than the last, and
  offers you one. It's too fancy for any house you own. She notices and laughs at herself.
- **4 hearts (a letter):** the firm that bought Corrie Glass is putting the Corrie name on cheap mass-made
  panes. She's furious, then quiet: "It's not ours any more. That was the point."
- **6 hearts (the Hall):** she pitches her designs for the Hall's windows to Lupini. If the Hall hasn't
  reopened, she pitches them to you on the plaza, looking up at its dusty windows.
- **8 hearts (her first panel):** the first panel she makes entirely by hand in twenty years cracks in the
  kiln. She admits what she's afraid of. Jet is in the doorway; he heard.
- **10 hearts (morning light):** the first of the Hall's windows goes in. Early morning, the three of you
  in the empty Hall, the colour moving across the floor. If the Hall hasn't reopened, it's the window of
  Bea's tea room instead, with Bea.

**Jet**
- **2 hearts (the seedhouse):** at dawn he's replacing a cracked pane in Fern's seedhouse. He hands you an
  offcut of blue glass and says one sentence.
- **4 hearts (Mabel's bench):** he won't sit at it. He tells you about learning to cut glass at her knee.
- **6 hearts (the loft):** clearing the studio loft, he finds Mabel's pattern book, left behind when they
  boarded up, with one window she never finished.
- **8 hearts (the line):** the twins argue across the line on the floor, for real this time. You help them
  find a way to share the middle; the line gets a gap. He finally says he never wanted to leave.
- **10 hearts (the window):** he sits at Mabel's bench for the first time and finishes her design. It goes
  into the studio's own window.

## The interior through their hearts *(Claude's call: which hearts)*
- From Opal's 6: their own glass lamps replace the city ones.
- From Jet's 8: the gap in the line.
- From Jet's 10: Mabel's bench in use; her window in theirs.
