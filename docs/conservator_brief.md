# Character brief: Margot Pell, the conservator (DRAFT, for approval)

The second romance (1.3). Decided so far (2026-11-01): she's the conservator
from the Zuzu City museum, a woman, marriageable by any farmer, and she lives
at the Saloon when she arrives, then moves into a home of her own partway
through her story.

## At a glance
- **Name:** **Margot Pell** (decided 2026-11-01).
- **Pronouns:** she/her.
- **Marriage:** yes, by any farmer, like Sable.
- **Age:** early thirties, about Sable's age.
- **Birthday:** Spring 19 (decided 2026-11-01; free in vanilla and in Stardew Valley Expanded, a week after the Egg Festival).
- **Home (decided 2026-11-01):** a room of her own when she arrives, down a short flight of steps from Gus's storeroom upstairs at the Saloon (the Saloon's only bedroom is Gus's), like the steps down to Sebastian's room. Its furniture is new art made for her: a restoration table with a magnifying lamp, a plan chest, crates and wrapped paintings from the city museum, her travelling trunk. From her 4-heart event (decided 2026-11-01: early, so the cottage is her home for a good while before any marriage), **a cottage of her own in town, built by Robin** (an event with Robin at the building site), which she makes into her home and a little conservation studio. Her Saloon room then becomes **a guest room** (Gus redoes it, with new art).
- **Role in the mod:**
  - looks after the old paintings that start turning up in the valley (restoration: 1.3's Mastery)
  - works at the museum with Gunther
  - a romance with its own story, woven through Sable's

## Look
- **Build:** small and quick, always slightly bent over something. Steady hands.
- **Hair:** dark auburn, pinned up untidily with a pencil through it.
- **Face:** freckled, warm brown eyes, often a jeweller's loupe pushed up on her forehead.
- **Clothes:** a sage-green work apron with deep pockets over a cream blouse, sleeves rolled to the elbow, brown corduroy trousers. White cotton gloves sticking out of a pocket.
- **Palette:** sage, cream and brass: museum colours. Warm beside Sable's teal and rust, soft beside Julian's charcoal.
- **Not like Penny or Maru:** Penny is gentle and shy; Maru is a scientist in a lab coat. She's practical, talkative and a bit messy, happiest with her hands busy.

## Backstory
- **The museum:** she trained in the Zuzu City Museum of Art's conservation studio, a few years behind Sable. Sable was the one who taught her how to clean a varnish without lifting the paint.
- **The painting:** it was her signature on the paperwork that sent the forest landscape signed "E." to Sable for restoration. When Sable quit and kept it ("borrowed, indefinitely"), it was her mess to explain.
- **Why she comes:** old paintings have started turning up in the valley, in the mines and in the ground, and the museum sends a conservator to see what they are. It also asks her, quietly, to bring the "E." landscape back.
- **The arc:** she arrives to do a job and settle a debt. She falls for the valley (and maybe you), finds out who "E." was, and decides the painting belongs where it is. She stays, and makes the valley a place where old paintings are looked after.

## Personality and voice
- Warm, curious and talkative, especially about her work. She explains things nobody asked about, then apologises, then keeps going.
- Patient with paintings, impatient with people who rush them.
- **Inside:** she's spent her life fixing other people's work and never made her own. She's not sure she'd be any good, and doesn't want to find out.
- **Speech:** quick, with a habit of describing people as if they were paintings ("You've got good bones. Structurally. Not that I was looking.").

Sample lines:
- First meeting: "Oh! You're the farmer who paints. I'm with the museum, the city one. I'm here for the old paintings. Have you found any? You'd know. They smell like linseed and regret."
- On her work: "You don't make it new. You make it able to keep being old."
- On Sable: "Sable taught me everything worth knowing. Including how to leave without telling anyone."
- Later: "I've touched a thousand paintings. I've never made one. Isn't that strange?"

## Sable, Julian and the others
- **Sable:** warm and wary at once. They were friends; Sable leaving hurt. The "E." painting sits between them until her 6-heart event.
- **Julian:** she's read the review and is unimpressed by him; he's unimpressed by "restorers". They bicker at the Saloon on his visit days, and come to like each other.
- **Gunther:** her ally. He gives her a corner of the museum to work in.
- **Evelyn:** once the "E." story is known (Sable's 8-heart event), she visits Evelyn to ask about the painting.

## Arrival and schedule
- **When she arrives (decided 2026-11-01):** the week after you find your first damaged old painting. Gunther writes that the city museum is sending someone. Damaged paintings can turn up from early on, so she can arrive in year one; restoring them yourself comes with Painting Mastery.
- **Weekdays:** the museum, at her table, from 9 to 5; the Saloon in the evening.
- **Sundays:** sketching by the river or at the beach (badly; she won't show you).
- **Rain:** the museum all day.
- **After she moves:** her cottage in the evening instead of the Saloon.

## Heart events (outline; scripts to be written for approval)
- **2 hearts (the museum):** she's working on a damaged old painting under a lamp, and shows you how inpainting works: matching what's missing to what's left.
- **4 hearts (the river):** she's looking for the exact spot an old painting was painted from. You find it together.
- **6 hearts (the Saloon, her room):** she admits she was sent to bring the "E." landscape back. A choice: tell her to report it, to talk to Sable, or that it's not your business.
- **8 hearts (the museum, after hours):** she tells the museum the painting couldn't be found, and decides to stay. She asks Robin to build her a cottage in town; a short scene at the building site with Robin follows, and she moves in (her home changes from here).
- **10 hearts (her studio, dating):** she shows you the drawing under one of the old paintings (the artist changed their mind), and asks to paint something of her own. With you.
- **14 hearts (married):** the first old painting the two of you restored hangs in the museum, with both your names on the card.

## Marriage
- **Spouse room:** her restoration desk, with a lamp, a magnifier and a painting under repair.
- **Patio:** a drying rack of small canvases: she's started painting her own.
- **Married life:** she keeps her museum hours, and sometimes brings home an old painting to show you.
