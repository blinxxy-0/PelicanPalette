# Character brief: Tess Brand, the woodcarver (DRAFT, for approval)

1.4, the Gilded Vale. Decided so far (the user, 2026-11-08): a woman in her early twenties, a woodcarver,
granddaughter of Bram Fairlie, who carved in the Vale's Studio III for 31 summers and is buried in the Vale.
She never met him. She arrives under her father's surname and keeps the link quiet until a heart event
reveals it. Marriageable by any farmer, 1.4's third romance. The sale and renovation around her:
`docs/design/vale_newcomers.md`.

Lines marked *(Claude's call)* are Claude's drafting, open to change.

## At a glance
- **Name:** **Tess Brand** *(the user)*. Brand is her father's name; her mother was Bram Fairlie's daughter.
- **Pronouns:** she/her.
- **Marriage:** yes, by any farmer *(the user)*, like Sable, Margot, Fern and Emmett.
- **Age:** early twenties.
- **Birthday:** **Fall 25** *(Claude's call: free in vanilla and the mod, no festival; to check against
  SVE)*. When the Vale's trees have dropped their gold and the wood shows.
- **Home and work:** Studio III of the Old Studios, Bram's old studio.
- **Arrival:** Winter 1, Year 3, with the first snow, after the For Sale notice (Summer, Year 3), the Sold
  notice (Fall) and Robin's renovation *(the user)*. In her first week she carves a new III over the door
  herself, plain and ungilded *(the user)*.
- **Role in the mod:**
  - sells carved and, later, built wooden furniture at her counter: new pieces (carved figures and
    animals, stools, frames; no signs) beside a selection of the game's own wooden furniture *(the user)*
  - the Vale's youngest resident, and the first of its grandchildren to come back
  - her story is how the player finds out the Vale's past isn't finished

## Look *(Claude's call)*
- **Build:** small and quick, with a carver's forearms.
- **Hair:** dark, chin-length, pushed back with whatever's to hand (usually a pencil, sometimes a chisel).
- **Face:** pale, wind-reddened cheeks, grey eyes, a straight serious mouth that surprises everyone when it
  smiles.
- **Clothes:** a moss-green knitted jumper with the elbows gone, canvas trousers, boots. **A too-big
  leather apron** she found in the studio: Bram's, though nobody knows that yet.
- **Details:** sawdust in her hair; a roll of chisels tied with string; plasters on two fingers.
- **Palette:** walnut, moss and snow: Studio III in winter. Darker and quieter than Fern's meadow colours.
- **Not like Leah or Robin:** Leah sculpts and forages, Robin builds houses. Tess carves small things and
  learns to build furniture, and she's starting out, not established.

## Backstory
- **Bram:** her mother's father, a carver in Studio III for 31 summers, then at home after he handed the
  studio to Ida Venn. He died about twenty years ago, when Tess was a baby, and is buried in the Vale
  *(the user)* under the one plain, uncarved stone, with a glazed urn at its foot (the graveyard design:
  nobody was left to carve his).
- **What came down to her *(the user)*:** his chisels and his letters. Her mother never carved and rarely
  talked about the Vale. Tess taught herself from the tools and the letters, which describe the studio,
  the light, the other artists, and a carving he never finished.
- **Why she comes:** when Studio III came up for sale, she spent everything she had on it. She doesn't
  tell anyone why.
- **Why she hides it *(Claude's call)*:** she wants to know if she belongs in the Vale as herself, not as
  Bram's granddaughter.
- **The arc *(Claude's call)*:** whether she's in the Vale for him or for herself. The answer she finds is
  both: she finishes his carving, and the furniture she builds in his studio is her own.

## Personality and voice *(Claude's call)*
- Serious, private, stubborn; very funny once she trusts you, in a dry, sideways way.
- Proud: she won't take help, then quietly accepts it later and pays it back in carved things.
- **Inside:** she's afraid she's only good because of his tools, and that the Vale will love Bram's
  granddaughter and not her.
- **Speech:** short, careful sentences; she thinks before she answers; she names wood by smell.

Sample lines:
- First meeting: "Mind the shavings. I'm not sweeping them. They're insulation."
- On her work: "You don't carve a shape into wood. You find out which shape it'll let you have."
- Before the reveal, about the studio: "Whoever was here before me knew what they were doing. Every tool's
  where your hand goes."
- After the reveal: "Everyone keeps telling me stories about him. I like the ones where he was wrong."

## The others *(Claude's call)*
- **Lupini:** he notices the way she holds a chisel *(the user)* and goes quiet. He knew Bram for years.
- **Jet Corrie:** he grew up next door to Bram and recognises the tools first. He keeps her secret until
  she's ready.
- **Opal Corrie:** wants to feed her. Tess lets her, eventually.
- **Robin:** the first person in the Vale to see her use a chisel, while renovating the studio. Later she
  sells Tess timber and teaches her joinery, which is how Tess learns to build furniture.
- **Tobin:** they trade: a carved handle for one of his mugs. He's the only one who doesn't ask questions.
- **Fern:** brings plants into the studio until it's warm enough to keep them alive.
- **Emmett:** he asks to hear about Bram for a song. She says no, then gives him a letter to read.
- **Bea:** gives her credit at the inn through that first broke winter.
- **Leah (in 1.5):** two people who work in wood. Leah teaches sculpting; Tess is the one who builds the
  stand it goes on.

## Schedule *(Claude's call)*
- **Fine days:** the studio in the morning; afternoons gathering fallen timber in the Vale's woods.
- **Rain:** the studio all day.
- **Tuesdays:** the bus to Pelican Town and Robin's shop, for timber and joinery.
- **Winter:** the studio most of the day; at dusk, a walk past the graveyard she doesn't go into until her
  8-heart event.

## Gift tastes (first pass) *(Claude's call)*
- **Paintings:** the mod's rule, by quality. An **art lover**.
- **Loves:** Hardwood, Mahogany Seed, Pine Tar, Maple Syrup, Pumpkin Soup.
- **Likes:** Wood, Oak Resin, cooked food (she's broke), anything carved.
- **Dislikes:** Sap ("it's not a present, it's a mess"), anything plastic-looking from Joja.

## Heart events (outline; scripts to be written for approval) *(Claude's call)*
- **2 hearts (the numeral, again):** in the snow, on a ladder, re-cutting the III she carved in her first
  week: it isn't right yet. She won't let you hold the ladder. Then she lets you.
- **4 hearts (Lupini):** you and Lupini visit; he watches her work and goes quiet at how she holds the
  chisel *(the user)*. After he's gone she asks you what he knows.
- **6 hearts (the reveal) *(the user)*:** she shows you Bram's initials stamped on her tools and tells you
  who she is. That evening the Vale knows (Jet first, who already guessed). Bram's photograph comes out of
  the drawer.
- **8 hearts (the graveyard):** she goes to Bram's grave for the first time, with you: the plain stone
  nobody carved. She reads him one of his own letters, and asks if she may carve his stone. She does
  *(the user)*: from then on Bram's stone is carved, in her own style.
- **10 hearts (dating):** the chair. The first piece of furniture she's built that isn't in his style.
  She asks you to sit in it first. Bram's carving is finished *(Claude's call: at 10, her last friendship-
  arc event, so players who date her see the carving finished)*.
- **14 hearts (married):** she carves something for the farm: a sign with both your names, in the style
  she's found for herself.

## Marriage *(Claude's call)*
- **Spouse room:** a workbench, timber stacked by kind, Bram's letters in a box she made, and a small
  carving of the Vale on the shelf.
- **Patio:** a carving bench and a half-finished piece that changes with the seasons.
- **Married life:** two days a week she takes the bus back to Studio III to work; it stays her workshop and
  counter.
