Change these with [Generic Mod Config Menu](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/5098), or in `Mods/PelicanPalette/PelicanPalette/config.json` after the game has run once.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `Difficulty` | `Normal` | **Relaxed:** no light meter, no paint needed, and each colour only needs mixing once.<br>**Normal:** mixed colours dry after a while.<br>**Hard:** the paint on the brush runs out, the light never pauses, and sparks fade fast; paintings pay 20% more. |
| `LightTimeMultiplier` | `1.0` | Gives you more (or less) light per painting, on top of the difficulty (0.5x to 3x). |
| `SimplePictures` | `false` | References in plain paint only, with no glazes or highlights to copy, in any difficulty (except for glazed commissions; the Glaze tool is there from a medium canvas). Easier, and worth a little less. Also on the easel's setup screen, above the reference. |
| `ArtLoversLikeAnyPainting` | `true` | Whether art lovers like even a normal-quality painting. |
