## Your first painting
1. Craft an **Easel** and place it anywhere, indoors or out.
2. Make sure you have a **Small Canvas** and a **Palette** in your bag. Sable gives you a Palette on your first visit to the [Art Shop](Art-Shop).
3. Right-click the easel. Pick the canvas size, then something from your bag to paint.
4. Press **Begin** and copy the reference on the left of the screen. See [Painting](Painting) for how mixing and the light work.
5. Finish, and the painting goes into your bag.

## What next
- **Visit the [Art Shop](Art-Shop)**, west of the path down from Marnie's ranch. Sable sells canvases, paint and brushes, and buys paintings for more than Pierre does.
- **Check the commission board** in the shop. Villagers ask for paintings of things they love; see [Commissions](Commissions).
- **Level up** your [Painting Skill](Painting-Skill). It brings bigger canvases, new tools and higher quality.
- **Learn more colours** ([Pigments and Paint](Pigments-and-Paint)). The more colours you know, the closer your paintings get to the real thing, and the more they're worth.
