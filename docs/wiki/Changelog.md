## 1.3.0

- **Margot Pell, the conservator.** A restorer from the Zuzu City museum arrives the week after you find your first damaged old painting, and she's a second romance (marriageable by any farmer). She starts in a room down the steps from Gus's storeroom at the Saloon and works at the museum. Six heart events, from a painting with a gate missing to both your names on a museum card. At 4 hearts Robin builds her a cottage by the river, with a big north window, a workroom and a little kitchen, and her Saloon room becomes Gus's guest room. Married, she has her own corner of the farmhouse, an easel and a rack of her own small canvases on the patio, her own wedding, and her own lines.
- **Julian and Margot.** On one of his visit days, if you've met them both, they meet at the Saloon and argue about whether restoring a painting is art, and you settle it. After that they keep bickering on his visits, a little fonder each time.
- **Painting Mastery and restoration.** Past Painting level 10, a mastery bar fills as you paint. When it's full, Margot sets you a test restoration at the museum; restore it well and you're a conservator: the Master's Easel (more light), and the right to restore old paintings yourself. Before then, Margot restores them for you, for a fee.
- **Old paintings.** Twenty-one damaged old paintings in five sets turn up around the valley: in artifact spots (the Old Valley), fishing treasure (the Wreck), mine crates (the Miners), the Skull Cavern (Calico) and Volcano chests (the Dwarves), and now and then at the Travelling Cart. The first is guaranteed from Painting level 3. Restore one at the easel and it's worth far more than your own work.
- **The museum's collection.** Give Gunther a restored painting and it joins the museum's collection. Each set finished brings its own frame style (the Old Valley brings a velvet rope, a gallery bench and a picture light); six bring a living painting whose water shimmers; twelve bring Margot's loupe, worn like a ring, so old paintings turn up more often; all 21 bring a paint-tube Junimo from Sable.
- **The Painting Wing.** After your first donation, a new wing opens off the museum's exhibit hall: the Old Valley framed along the back wall, and every other painting on its own white marble display, with a place kept for each one still missing. Margot visits on Tuesday and Thursday afternoons.
- **Frames that do something.** Sixteen frame styles, from pine and oak to prismatic and Qi's, each with a rarity and a trait. A framed painting hung in a room helps paintings made at an easel there: more light, sparks sooner, paint saved, more XP, or more value (the three strongest frames in a room count). Sable sells the common ones, and more as your Painting level rises; the rarest come from the Night Market, the museum, the finished portfolio and Mr. Qi. The framing screen shows every frame you have, and what it does.
- **The Framer's Bench.** Takes a framed painting out of its frame overnight, so frames can be collected and swapped. Works with Automate.
- **The portfolio, finished.** A sixth page, Faces of the Valley (portraits of the town's groups and visitors). Finishing every page brings the gold-leaf frame, and then painting the view from the Summit brings Sable to the top with you to look through the book.
- **Julian buys a painting.** Once a season, on a visit day, he'll buy one of your paintings at twice the shop's price, and tell you what he thinks of it. He also has a winter coat now, for his winter visits.
- **Animals.** Paint your pet, your horse and the farm animals you own, from any easel. The record has an Animals tab.
- **Festivals.** An easel at Spirit's Eve, in the heart of the haunted maze, and on the beach at the Dance of the Moonlight Jellies, each with a view you can only paint that night. If Sable is your secret friend at the Feast of the Winter Star, their gift is a painting.
- **Special orders.** Lewis wants three portraits for the town hall, Gus two paintings for the Saloon's walls, Leah a view of the Secret Woods, Elliott the beach at night, and Mr. Qi a Masterpiece of a legendary fish.
- **Relaxed keeps the whole picture.** Relaxed used to leave out glazes and highlights, so a grey ghost came out green and brown. Now it only takes away the clock and the paint, and glazes and highlights are there as on Normal. For the old plain-paint pictures, turn on **Simple pictures**, above the reference on the easel or in the config, in any mode. Thanks to Hiname for the feedback.
- **Zoom.** From a medium canvas, the mouse wheel zooms the canvas in, up to 3x, towards the mouse, and the reference zooms with it to show the same part of the picture. The arrow keys move round. Thanks to Hiname for asking.
- **The easel shows what your frames do.** At a placed easel, the setup screen shows an icon for each bonus the room's framed paintings give; hover one to see how much.
- **Fixed: subjects missing from the easel.** With a full bag, or many friends, monsters and animals at once, whatever didn't fit on the setup screen was left off, so some villagers couldn't be painted. The items and the portrait row now turn pages (arrows, or the mouse wheel).
- **Fixed: Sable's shop with Alec Revisited.** Alec's house patch (and its SVE patch) painted over half the shop; the shop now goes onto the forest last. Thanks to AZLeoM for the report.
- **Fixed: an invisible wall beside Sable's shop**, at the tip of the roof's eave.
- **Fixed: the birch frame looked like the white one** in the inventory; every frame's icon now shows its style's mark.
- **Fixed: a black block at the Fair.** Under the painting contest's entries, where an invisible prop sometimes drew black.
- **Fixed: a cut-off portfolio label.** "Ginger Island's beach" ran to three lines under its slot; it's now "Ginger Island's south", and slot labels shrink to fit.

## 1.2.0

- **The back room.** A door in the shop's back wall, marked "Artists only!" and with a lit window, leads to a back room, where you keep your work: the **portfolio book**, a Community Center for paintings (pages of slots, each asking for a particular painting, which stays there and shows in its slot; a better one can replace it later), and the **plan chest**, a big chest's worth of drawers for any paintings you want to keep. Both are shared by the whole farm. Finishing a page brings a letter from Sable with a reward. Pages so far: The Four Seasons, Harvest and Fine Work.
- **Creatures.** Paint a study of any monster you've slain, from memory: the easel's setup screen has a creatures slot that shows them in place of your bag. Junimos join once the Community Center (or Joja's) is done. The record has a Creatures tab.
- **Krobus and the Wizard sit for portraits sooner:** once their stories are done (meeting Krobus in the sewers; bringing the Wizard the magic ink), without the usual 4 hearts.
- **Two more portfolio pages:** Far Places (the desert, the Skull Cavern, Ginger Island's beach and north, the Secret Woods) and Legends (the five legendary fish). You can now set up an easel just inside the Skull Cavern.
- **Julian Vane, the critic.** A city art critic with a history with Sable visits the valley the 22nd to the 24th of every season, off the morning bus and back on the evening one: your portfolio in the back room, the museum, the beach, the Saloon. Not marriageable; his friendship doesn't fade between visits. Five heart events tell his story with Sable, from your portfolio to a second review, with an Old Catalogue to deliver and a framed review to hang.
- **The Fair's painting contest.** Julian judges a painting contest at the Stardew Valley Fair, on the grass just past the grange row. Talk to him before Lewis judges the grange to enter one painting from your bag; it stands on his easel for the day. The villagers' entries lean against the tree beside him, and the tourist nearby has come all the way from Zuzu City to see it. Your painting is scored on its quality, stars, subject, varnish and whether it's a Masterpiece, against Leah, Elliott (or Evelyn, later in Sable's story) and Emily. After the judging, talk to him again for your placing, star tokens (1000, 500, 200, or 50 for entering), a blue, red or yellow ribbon to hang, and your painting back.
- **Sable at the Night Market.** All three nights from 5pm, Sable keeps a little skiff of their own, moored below the bridge to the submarine. They sell the **Moonlit Frame** (one a night): frame a painting in it at an easel and it hangs in midnight blue and silver. And they buy unusual paintings for half as much again, up to three a night: views of the Far Places, the legendary fish, monsters, and views painted at night or in the rain.
- **Views remember the night and the rain.** A view painted from 7pm, or in the rain, says so in its description.
- **Your portfolio at the gallery evening.** At Sable's exhibition (their 14-heart event), your portfolio book comes out of the back room on its lectern. A guest who finds their own portrait in it says so; otherwise Leah finds your best painting and names it; and if the book's still empty, Sable has put out your sketches.
- **Stardew Valley Expanded.** Everything new fits SVE's maps: with SVE, Julian's Fair table stands on the grass by the lamp, left of the north road (SVE's stalls fill the corner by the grange), and the tourist keeps SVE's own line.
- **Sable's trapdoor moved to the back room**, so the shop floor is clear.
- **The painting record shows your own painting** of a subject, where one's kept in the portfolio or the plan chest.
- **Softer outlines on the shop's furniture**, a darker shade of each piece's own colour instead of near-black.
- **The painting record has tabs.** Crops, Forage, Fish, Minerals, Cooking, Portraits, Views and everything else, like the game's Collections tab. What you haven't painted yet shows faded, with how to paint it. Painting a quarter, half and all of a tab brings rewards: paint, Picture Frames, and 10% more for that tab's paintings.

## 1.0.0 (first release)

**Painting**
- An easel, placed anywhere, opens the painting minigame:
  - Mix colours on a fishing-style bar.
  - Copy a reference cell by cell before the light runs out.
  - Catch inspiration sparks for bonus light, and an Inspired painting.
- Paint still lifes of anything in your bag (one to three items), portraits of friends at 4+ hearts, the view around an outdoor easel, or a free original.
- Small, medium and large canvases. Medium canvases add glazing and large ones add highlights, so the minigame grows with your skill.
- Quality from normal to iridium, decided by accuracy and skill. A perfect painting on linen becomes a **Masterpiece**.
- Paint is a resource:
  - You learn colours like recipes, and spend paint from your Palette.
  - Tubes go straight into your Palette, bought from Sable or ground in a Pigment Mill.
  - Wooden, Enamel and Iridium Palettes hold 3, 6 or 10 tubes of each colour. Set one down to store paint. Like tools, palettes can't be given away by accident.
- Five brushes, from hog bristle to iridium, that wear like fishing tackle, with a green wear bar like the watering can's.
- Painting takes time from your day: an hour for a small canvas, more for bigger ones.

**The Painting skill**
- Ten levels with perks, new tools, canvases and pigments.
- Professions at levels 5 and 10: Plein Air, Steady Hand, Visionary, Gallerist, Patron's Favorite, Prolific.

**Selling, gifting and keeping**
- Sell to Pierre, at the art shop (20% more) or through the shipping bin. Or consign up to three paintings at Sable's gallery, which sell over a few days for more again.
- A new subject sells for more. The same one painted over and over in a week sells for less, until Monday. Your painting record keeps every subject, with milestone rewards at 5, 15, 30 and 50.
- Everyone loves an iridium painting. Art lovers like any painting, nobody dislikes one, and a portrait's sitter always loves it.
- Frame paintings to hang them as furniture. Varnish them in a Varnishing Cabinet, which cures them like a cask: satin, gloss, gallery finish.

**The art shop and Sable**
- An art supply shop in Cindersap Forest, with a stocked, lamp-lit interior and a commission board where villagers ask for paintings (portraits too).
- Sable (they/them), a former museum restorer who runs it:
  - a full schedule, seasonal and island outfits, and every festival
  - hundreds of lines of dialogue
  - heart events at 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 and 14 hearts
  - dating and marriage, with their own spouse room and patio

**Compatibility**
- Stardew Valley 1.6.15, SMAPI 4.0+, Content Patcher 2.9+, SpaceCore.
- Works with Stardew Valley Expanded and in co-op multiplayer.
- Safe to add to an existing save.
