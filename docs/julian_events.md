# Julian Vane's heart events (DRAFT, for approval)

This builds on his brief (`docs/julian_brief.md`) and fits around Sable's story (`docs/phase6_romance.md`). The arc in one line: a critic who wrote one cruel, famous sentence about Sable ten years ago finally says what he should have said.

The thread through all five events is the catalogue of Sable's first show. In its margin, next to the third painting, Julian wrote "the third one is not empty", and never published it.

Portrait codes in the lines: `$h` happy, `$s` regret, `$u` his dry smirk, `$l` touched, `$a` annoyed, `$6` surprised, `$7` thoughtful.

**When they can happen:** only on his visit days (the 22nd to the 24th), where he is at that time. As with Sable's, each needs the one before it. The game walks every scene, and the player only answers the questions.

---

## 2 hearts: the third one (the back room, a visit morning)

**Trigger:** you enter the art shop's back room between 10am and 1pm on the 22nd or 24th, with 2+ hearts and at least three paintings kept in the portfolio book. Julian is at the book.

**Scene:**
- Julian turns a page of your portfolio slowly, holding his glasses in one hand. He doesn't look up. `$7` "Don't hover. Come and look with me."
- "This page. The first one is careful. The second is clever. The third..." A long pause. "Who painted the third one?"
- **Choice:**
  - "I did." → `$6` "Did you." He looks at you properly for the first time. "Hm." (+friendship)
  - "Sable taught me." → He goes very still. `$s` "...Did they." beat. "They were always a good teacher. Even when nobody asked." (+friendship)
- Sable comes in for a box of frames, sees him, and stops in the doorway. Neither speaks. Sable takes the box and leaves.
- Julian closes the book. `$u` "Well. That was the warmest welcome I've had in years."

**After:** his everyday lines start calling you by your name, not "the farmer".

## 4 hearts: an honest review (the museum, a visit afternoon)

**Trigger:** you enter the museum between 1pm and 4pm on the 22nd or 23rd, with 4+ hearts and the 2-heart event seen.

**Scene:**
- Julian stands in front of Gunther's empty display wall with his notebook open. `$u` "Your museum has room for exactly one painting. I've been deciding which of yours."
- He names your best kept painting (the mod fills in its title, as the gallery evening does) and reviews it aloud, honestly: one thing it does well and one thing it doesn't. The lines change with the painting's quality.
- `$7` "But that's what I think. What were you trying to do?"
- **Choice** (remembered, and it shapes his later lines):
  - "Get it exactly right." → `$0` "Then you did. Exactness is underrated. People only call it cold when they're afraid of it." (*exact*)
  - "Make it feel like the place." → `$h` "Then you did that too. That's the harder one." (*feeling*)
  - "I just like painting." → `$6` long pause. `$l` "...That's the best answer anyone's given me in twenty years." (*joy*)
- Gunther, from his desk: "We don't actually have a painting wall." `$a` "You do now."

## 6 hearts: the evening bus (the bus stop, the 24th, evening)

**Trigger:** you're at the bus stop between 5:30 and 7pm on the 24th, with 6+ hearts, the 4-heart event seen, and Sable's 6-heart event (the letter) seen.

**Scene:**
- The bus pulls away without him. Julian watches it go with his hands in his coat pockets. `$u` "I missed it. On purpose. Don't tell anyone, it would ruin me."
- "Ten years ago I reviewed a young painter's first show. I wrote one sentence about it that everyone still quotes. 'Technically flawless and completely empty.'"
- "I wrote it because it sounded good. I was right about the first half." `$s`
- He takes an old exhibition catalogue from inside his coat. In the margin, next to the third painting, in pencil: *the third one is not empty.*
- "I wrote that the same night. I never published it. It didn't sound as good."
- **Choice:**
  - "You have to tell Sable." → `$s` "I know. I've known for ten years. Knowing is the easy part."
  - "Why tell me?" → `$7` "Because you're the only person here who looks at both of us like we're worth looking at."
  - (Say nothing.) → He laughs once. `$h` "A critic's dream. An audience that doesn't argue."
- Lewis drives him back to the city in his truck that night. (Lewis is mentioned, not shown.)

## 8 hearts: the catalogue (the Saloon, a visit evening)

**Trigger:** you enter the Saloon between 4pm and 6pm on a visit day, with 8+ hearts and the 6-heart event seen.

**Scene:**
- Julian sits alone at a table with a glass of wine he hasn't touched. The catalogue lies in front of him.
- `$s` "I've tried to walk into that shop four times. I get as far as the yard."
- He slides the catalogue across the table. "Would you give them this? Not from me. Just... give it to them."
- **Choice:**
  - "I'll do it." → `$l` "Thank you." beat. "That's two people in this valley I've said that to. Don't let it get around." (+friendship)
  - "You should give it to them yourself." → `$a` "I know what I should do. I'm asking what you'll do." beat. `$s` "...Please."

**Reward:** the **Old Catalogue**, a quest item. Give it to Sable.

**Giving it to Sable** (a short scene at the shop):
- Under 4 hearts with Sable: `$a` "Where did you get this? ...Put it in the stove." Sable takes it anyway.
- 4+ hearts: Sable turns to the third painting and reads the margin for a long time. `$6` "...'Not empty.'" beat. `$s` "Ten years. He wrote this ten years ago." They close it gently. "Thank you for bringing it. I don't know what I'll do with it yet."

## 10 hearts: the second review (the art shop, after hours, a visit day)

**Trigger:** you enter the shop between 6pm and 8pm on a visit day, with 10+ hearts, the 8-heart event seen, the catalogue given to Sable, and Sable's 8-heart event ("E.") seen, so they're painting again.

**Scene:**
- The shop is half dark. Sable stands at an easel with a new painting of their own: the river bend, painted with Evelyn. Julian stands at the door, and this time he comes in.
- Sable doesn't turn around. "You can say it. You've had ten years to work on it." `$a` (Sable)
- Julian takes out his notebook and reads from it, the review he means to publish. It mentions the river, the light on the water and the third painting from ten years ago. It ends: `$l` "Ten years ago I called this painter's work technically flawless and completely empty. I was wrong. This painting might not be technically flawless, but it is perfect in its own right."
- A long silence.
- Sable, `$u`: "'Perfect in its own right.' You'd never have written that ten years ago." beat. "...Thank you, Julian."
- (Reworded 2026-10-31 at the user's request: the first draft's "It is not technically flawless. It is completely full" didn't make sense. Julian now says plainly that the painting may not be flawless, but it's perfect in its own right, in the user's words.)
- Julian, `$h`: "I'll be back next season. To review the next one."
- If you're married to Sable, there's one more line from Sable: "Stay for dinner. The farmer cooks. Badly." (`$u`)

**After:**
- Sable's lines on his visit days change, from "I'll be at my easel" to "Julian's in town. He's buying the coffee this time."
- If you've seen Sable's gallery evening (14 hearts, married), Julian is among the guests the next time it shows. (He'd get one line there; it's an optional extra, done last.)

**Reward:** a framed copy of the review, as wall decor.
