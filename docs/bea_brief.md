# Character brief: Bea Thistle, the innkeeper (DRAFT, for approval)

1.4, the Gilded Vale. Decided so far (2026-11-03): a woman, a friend to ten hearts (not marriageable), renamed from the concept sheet's Mara Bell (too close to Margot Pell, and then Maru).

## At a glance
- **Name:** Bea Thistle (decided 2026-11-03). Short for Beatrice, which only her great-aunt ever called her.
- **Pronouns:** she/her.
- **Marriage:** no. Friendship to 10 hearts.
- **Age:** about forty.
- **Birthday:** Spring 22 (free in vanilla; to check against SVE).
- **Home and work:** the Vale House, the Vale's inn and tea room. She lives in the rooms upstairs.
- **Role in the mod:**
  - the Vale's social hub: the inn, the tea room, the noticeboard
  - sells food, tea and a room for the night (you can stay over, and take the morning bus home)
  - hosts the Open House's refreshments and Emmett's evenings by the fire

## Look
- **Build:** sturdy, always moving, sleeves pushed up.
- **Hair:** a thick chestnut bob, tucked behind her ears, a pencil through it on busy days.
- **Face:** rosy, sharp hazel eyes, a dry half-smile.
- **Clothes:** a heather-purple dress with a striped linen apron, a tea towel over one shoulder.
- **Details:** a ring of old keys on her belt (the inn has more doors than guests).
- **Palette:** heather, plum and linen: a thistle's colours. Warm beside the Vale's greens and golds.
- **Not like Gus or Emily:** Gus is jolly and fatherly; Emily is dreamy. Bea is brisk, funny, and tired in a cheerful way.

## Backstory
- **The inn:** her great-aunt ran the Vale House for fifty years, when the Vale was full of artists. Bea inherited it at thirty-two and meant to sell it within a year. That was eight years ago.
- **Before:** she was a hotel manager in Zuzu City, at a big hotel where nobody knew anyone's name.
- **The trouble:** she's so used to looking after other people that she never asks what she wants. The inn's old, half-empty, and costs more than it makes.
- **The arc:** she decides whether to keep the inn, and finds it can be hers rather than her aunt's.

## Personality and voice
- Warm, sociable, quick-witted, and quietly sarcastic.
- She knows everything about everyone, and keeps it to herself.
- She finds it funny that guests call the Vale "peaceful": they've never fixed its plumbing.
- **Speech:** brisk and dry, a joke at the end of every complaint.

Sample lines:
- First meeting: "A guest! Or a lost hiker. Either way, there's tea."
- On the inn: "It has eleven rooms, two of them with roofs."
- On Lupini: "He's run a tab since before I was born. I'm told it's an honour."
- On Gus (once she's met him): "A man who understands that the kitchen is the only room that matters."
- Later: "Do you know what nobody asks an innkeeper? What she'd like for dinner."

## The others
- **Emmett:** she hosts his evenings by the fire; they're old friends, and squabble like it.
- **Fern:** Fern brings flowers for the tea room; Bea complains they wilt in a day ("they're wildflowers, Bea").
- **Tobin:** the inn's cups are all his. She says they're too heavy, and won't use any others.
- **Lupini:** his tab.
- **Gus:** she visits the Saloon after the Vale opens. They swap recipes.

## Schedule
- **Every day:** the Vale House: the tea room from 9, the bar in the evening.
- **Wednesdays:** the bus to Pelican Town in the morning, for Pierre's; back by 2.
- **Sundays:** the tea room closes at noon; she walks the riverside.
- **The Open House:** the refreshments table in the plaza.

## Gift tastes (first pass)
- **Loves:** a good tea (Green Tea, the Tea Set), Pink Cake, anything that's cooked for *her*.
- **Likes:** fruit, flowers, honey, eggs and milk ("for the kitchen").
- **Dislikes:** anything that makes more work: clay, sap, trash.

## Heart events (outline; scripts to be written for approval)
- **2 hearts (the tea room):** a coach party cancels; she's made food for twelve. You eat some, and she tells you how she ended up here.
- **4 hearts (the inn's attic):** she asks you to help clear it. Her great-aunt's guest books, fifty years of artists' signatures and sketches. She can't throw them away.
- **6 hearts (the plaza):** a buyer from the city offers for the inn. She asks what you'd do. A choice that doesn't decide it: she's asking herself.
- **8 hearts (the Vale House, closed for the night):** she cooks something for herself, for the first time in years, and asks you to stay. She turns the buyer down.
- **10 hearts (the inn, reopened):** she opens the Vale House her way: the guest books on the shelf for anyone to read, a room kept free for artists who can't pay. She gives you a key to it (you can stay free).

## After her arc
- The guest-book shelf in the tea room; the artists' room upstairs.
- Visiting artists turn up now and then, as small, one-off scenes.
