# Opal and Jet Corrie: gift tastes and everyday dialogue (batch 1, approved by the user 2026-11-09)

Batch 1 of the twins' writing (`docs/corrie_twins_brief.md`). Keys as the other residents'
(`data/tobin_dialogue.json`): day names, with heart levels (Mon4 = from 4 hearts, the highest
reached wins), seasons, first days, the birthday, rain, gifts.

Portraits: 0 neutral, `$h` happy, `$s` sad, `$u` unique (Opal laughing out loud; Jet one brow up,
dry), `$l` touched, `$a` angry.

Left out of this batch on purpose, for later batches with conditions: the Exhibition Hall (its
windows are their first big job only once Lupini's 8-heart event reopens it), Tess (she arrives
Winter 1, Year 3), and anything their heart events reveal (Opal's fear about her hands is her
8 hearts; "I never wanted to leave" is Jet's 8). Nobody names Studio I's painter.
