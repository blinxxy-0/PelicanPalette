# Tess Brand's heart events (2, 4 and 6 approved by the user 2026-11-09 and built: tools/build_tess_events.py)

From the approved brief (`docs/tess_brief.md`, heart events outline) and design
(`docs/design/vale_newcomers.md`). This draft is her **2-, 4- and 6-heart events**; the 8-, 10- and
14-heart events, the wedding and married life come in the next batch.

Portrait codes are her dialogue's: `$0` neutral, `$h` happy, `$s` sad, `$u` her dry half-smile, `$l` love,
`$a` angry. Event IDs are fixed already (the studio follows them): `blinxxy.MuseArt_TessHeart2`, `...4`, `...6`.
Each needs the one before it. They happen where her schedule already has her, so nobody has to be moved.

**What they change, already built:** Bram's carving moves on after each event (sheet off at 2, first leaves at
4, half the crown at 6); the room stage changes after 4 (the rug, Fern's fern); after 6 his photographs go up
on the wall and the locked drawer opens; her 6-heart lines and Pine Tar's second line need the 6-heart event.

## 2 hearts: the numeral, again (outside Studio III, a morning)

**Trigger:** you enter the Gilded Vale between 8am and 11am, not raining, with 2+ hearts, once the carved III
is up (from Winter 7, Year 3).

**Scene:**
- Tess is up a stepladder against Studio III's front, a mallet in one hand, a gouge in the other, re-cutting
  the III she carved over the door. (In winter: snow on her shoulders and on the ladder's top step.)
- You come close. `$0` "Don't stand under me. Chips." #$b# "It's not right. The middle stroke's thinner than
  the other two. Nobody'd notice. I notice."
- The ladder wobbles. She grabs the frame. `$a` "I'm fine."
- **Choice:**
  - "Let me hold the ladder." → `$a` "I said I'm fine." The ladder wobbles again. A pause. `$u` "...Hold the
    ladder."
  - "It looks right to me." → `$u` "That's because you're not the one who carved it." The ladder wobbles. `$0`
    "Hold the ladder. Please."
  - (Hold the ladder without asking.) → `$s` She opens her mouth to argue. Shuts it. Keeps carving.
- A few strokes. She climbs down, stands back beside you, looks up at it for a long time.
- `$h` "There. Now it's mine." #$b# `$0` "...Thanks. For the ladder. Don't tell anyone I needed it."

**After:** her 2-heart lines; the carving's dust sheet comes off (already built).

## 4 hearts: the way he holds it (Studio III, a morning)

**Trigger:** you enter Studio III between 8am and 11am (she's at her bench), not on a Sunday, with 4+ hearts
and her 2-heart event seen.

**Scene:**
- Lupini comes in behind you, hat in hand, all apologies and flourish. `$u` (Lupini) "Forgive me, forgive me.
  The farmer said the new carver doesn't bite. I have been meaning to come and admire."
- Tess, not looking up: `$0` "The farmer says a lot of things. Mind the shavings."
- Lupini watches her work. She's taking long curls off a block of ash with a gouge, her thumb laid along the
  blade. He stops talking, which he never does.
- After a while: (Lupini, quietly, `$s`) "You hold it as if you were apologising to the wood." #$b# "I knew
  someone who held a chisel like that. A long time ago." He puts his hat back on. "Good morning, Miss Brand. It
  is very good work."
- He goes. Tess has stopped carving.
- `$0` "What does he know?" #$b# "He looked at my hands like he'd seen them before."
- **Choice:**
  - "He's been in the Vale thirty years. He knew the old carvers." → `$s` "...Right. Of course he did." She
    turns the gouge over in her fingers.
  - "Ask him yourself." → `$a` "I'm not asking Lupini anything. He'd make a speech." A pause. `$u` "A long one."
  - "Maybe all carvers hold a chisel that way." → `$u` "Maybe." She doesn't believe it. Neither do you.
- She picks the gouge back up. `$0` "Stay if you like. Don't talk. Talking's for people who've finished."

**After:** her 4-heart lines; the room's first change (the rug, Fern's fern) and the carving's first leaves
(already built).

## 6 hearts: B.F. (Studio III, an evening)

**Trigger:** you enter Studio III between 7pm and 10pm (she's at her bench), with 6+ hearts and her 4-heart
event seen.

**Scene 1, the studio:**
- The middle drawer of the old dresser is open. Tess is sitting on the floor in front of it with a roll of
  chisels in her lap and a bundle of letters tied with string.
- `$0` "Sit down. I need to show you something, and if I stand up I won't."
- She hands you a chisel, handle first, and turns your hand so the lamplight catches the steel. Stamped into it,
  small and worn: **B.F.**
- `$s` "Bram Fairlie. Studio III, thirty-one summers. It's on the sign by the lane." #$b# "He was my grandfather.
  My mother's father. He died when I was a baby. I never met him."
- `$0` "Mum never carved. She hardly ever talked about the Vale. When she cleared out the attic she gave me a box
  and said I was the only one who'd want it. His chisels, and his letters to her. Years of letters, about this
  room. The light. The other artists. A tree he never finished."
  She looks at the carving in the corner.
- "When the studio came up for sale I spent everything I had. Everything. And then I didn't tell anyone."
- **Choice:**
  - "Why didn't you tell anyone?" → `$s` "Because then I'd be Bram's granddaughter. Everybody would be kind to
    me because of him." #$b# `$0` "I wanted to know if I'd be any good here as just me."
  - "Why are you telling me?" → `$l` "...Because you held the ladder." #$b# `$u` "Don't make it a thing."
  - (Say nothing.) → `$0` She waits. Then she laughs, once. `$u` "That's the right answer. That's annoyingly the
    right answer."
- She takes something else out of the drawer: a photograph in a plain frame. A big man in a leather apron on the
  step of Studio III, laughing at something out of frame. The apron is the one she's wearing.
- She hangs it on the wall by the dresser, next to the others she's kept hidden. `$0` "There. He can have his
  wall back."

**Scene 2, the Vale House, later:**
- The tea room, after dark. Bea is wiping the counter. Jet Corrie is at a table with a cup of tea. Opal is
  talking. Lupini is by the fire. Tess comes in behind you and stands in the doorway.
- `$0` "I've got something to say. It won't take long."
- Before she can, Jet puts down his cup. (Jet, `$0`) "Bram's chisels. I knew in your first week." #$b# "I
  grew up next door to him. He used to let me sweep."
- (Opal, `$a`) "You KNEW? Jet! You let me make her soup for a whole winter without telling me she was
  family?" #$b# (Jet) "You'd have made more soup."
- Tess's mouth does the thing it does when it surprises everyone. `$h`
- (Lupini, standing, glass raised, `$u`) "To Bram Fairlie, who once carved a whole chair to win an argument
  about chairs, and lost it." #$b# (Bea, `$h`) "And to his granddaughter, who still owes me for eleven
  breakfasts."
- `$u` "Twelve. I've been counting."

**After:** his photographs on the wall and the drawer open (already built); her after-the-reveal lines; the
carving's crown half done. From here the Vale's residents can talk about her as Bram's granddaughter (their
lines, later).
