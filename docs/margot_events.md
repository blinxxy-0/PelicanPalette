# Margot Pell's heart events (approved 2026-11-01)

This builds on her brief (`docs/conservator_brief.md`) and fits around Sable's story (`docs/phase6_romance.md`) and Julian's (`docs/julian_events.md`). The arc in one line: a conservator sent to fix things and fetch a painting back decides the valley is where she belongs, and makes something of her own.

The thread through the events is the **"E." landscape**. Margot's signature sent it to Sable for restoration years ago; when Sable quit and kept it, it became her problem. The museum has quietly asked her to bring it back.

Portrait codes in the lines: `$h` happy, `$s` sad, `$u` explaining (her own), `$l` flustered, `$a` annoyed, `$6` surprised, `$7` thoughtful.

**How they run:** each needs the one before it. The game walks every scene; the player only answers questions (your rule for cutscenes).

**Changed by the move (decided 2026-11-01):** she moves into her cottage at **4 hearts**, not 8, so the cottage is her home for a good while before any marriage. Robin builds it. The river scene that was at 4 hearts moves to 8.

## 2 hearts: what's missing (the museum, a weekday afternoon)

**Trigger:** you enter the museum between 1pm and 4pm on a weekday, with 2+ hearts. Margot is at the reading desks.

**Scene:**
- A small damaged painting lies on the desk under her magnifying lamp: a farmhouse, a third of it flaked away. She doesn't look up. `$u` "Come here. Quick, before the light moves."
- "Most people think restoring is painting over the damage. It isn't. You look at what's left until it tells you what's missing."
- She points with a brush handle. "The fence runs this way. The shadow's long, so it's evening. So this gap..." She waits for you.
- **Choice:**
  - "A gate." → `$6` "...Yes. A gate. How did you —" `$h` "You looked. That's all it is." (+friendship)
  - "Another fence post." → `$7` "That's what I thought too. Then I saw the path." She turns the lamp. "A gate." (+friendship, a little less)
  - "I'd have to see the rest." → `$u` "Good answer. Annoying, but good." (+friendship)
- She dabs one tiny stroke into the gap, then sits back. `$s` "I've touched a thousand paintings. I've never made one. Isn't that strange?"

**After:** her everyday lines start asking what you're painting.

## 4 hearts: a place of her own (Robin's shop, a weekday morning)

**Trigger:** you enter Robin's shop (ScienceHouse) between 9am and noon on a weekday, with 4+ hearts and the 2-heart event seen. Margot is at Robin's counter with a rolled-up drawing.

**Scene:**
- Robin, at the counter: "...so a big north window, a workroom, a little kitchen. That's a cottage, not a shed." Margot: `$u` "North light doesn't change all day. It's the only light you can trust with paint."
- She sees you. `$l` "Oh! You weren't supposed to see this yet. I mean — it's not a secret. It's just... a lot."
- `$7` "The museum sent me for a season. It's been more than that. Gus has started charging me for jam." beat. "I don't want to go back to a city where nothing's old enough to need me."
- Robin unrolls the drawing: a small cottage with a big window. Robin: "Three days, if the weather holds. Where are we putting it?"
- **Choice** (remembered; she mentions it in the cottage):
  - "Near the museum." → `$h` "Close to work. Sensible. Gunther will never let me leave." (*museum*)
  - "Near the river." → `$7` "Where the old painters sat. ...I like that." (*river*)
  - "Somewhere you can see the whole town." → `$u` "Then I'll know what needs fixing first." (*town*)
  (The choice changes her line, not where it's built: the plot is fixed; see open question 1.)
- Robin: "Three days, then." Margot, very quietly: `$l` "I'm staying. I said it out loud. Now I have to."

**After:** three days later a letter from Robin says it's done; from that morning Margot lives in her cottage. The first time you visit, she's unpacking crates and the restoration table is already set up under the window. Her Saloon room becomes Gus's guest room (new wallpaper and furniture).

## 6 hearts: the errand (her cottage, evening)

**Trigger:** you enter her cottage between 6pm and 9pm with 6+ hearts and the 4-heart event seen. **Also needs Sable's 6-heart event (the letter) seen**, so the museum's interest in Sable is already in the story.

**Scene:**
- Margot sits at her table with an unopened envelope from the city museum. She hasn't lit the lamp.
- `$s` "I have to tell someone, and you're the only one who won't make it worse."
- "Years ago I signed for a painting. A forest landscape signed 'E.' I sent it to Sable to be restored. Then Sable left the museum, and the painting left with them."
- "The museum didn't send me here for the old paintings. Well — not only. They asked me to bring it back. Quietly." `$a` "'Quietly.' As if I could do anything quietly."
- "Sable's been restoring it for a year. I've seen it through the shop window. It's nearly finished. It's beautiful." `$s`
- **Choice** (remembered; shapes the 8-heart event):
  - "Report it. It's your job." → `$7` "It is. ...It is my job." She doesn't open the envelope. (*report*)
  - "Talk to Sable first." → `$6` "Talk to them. Like a person." beat. `$h` "That's either very wise or very obvious." (*talk*)
  - "It's not my business." → `$u` "No. But you listened anyway. That's the nicest kind of not-your-business." (*not mine*)
- She props the envelope against the lamp, still sealed.

## 8 hearts: the river bend (the Forest river, a sunny morning)

**Trigger:** you enter the Forest between 8am and 11am on a sunny day, with 8+ hearts and the 6-heart event seen. Margot is on the riverbank holding a restored old painting (the Old Valley set's river scene, if you've found and restored it; otherwise one of hers from the city).

**Scene:**
- `$u` "Every painting was painted *from* somewhere. I've been looking for this one's spot for a week."
- She walks along the bank, holding the painting up, lowering it. The game walks you beside her.
- She stops. Holds it up. The bend, the willow, the light: it matches. `$6` "...Here. They stood exactly here."
- `$l` "A hundred years ago someone stood where we're standing and thought this was worth keeping."
- Then, depending on the 6-heart choice:
  - *report:* `$7` "I wrote to the museum. I told them the painting couldn't be recovered. It's true. It belongs where it is now."
  - *talk:* `$h` "I talked to Sable. They cried. I cried. Evelyn made us tea." (If Sable's 8-heart event has happened: "It's hers, you know. Evelyn's. 'E.'") "I've told the museum it's staying."
  - *not mine:* `$u` "I decided it's not my business either. I've told the museum it's staying where it's loved."
- `$s` "They'll stop sending me letters now. I'm just... a conservator who lives in a valley." beat. `$h` "I think that's the best thing I've ever been."

**After:** the envelope is gone from her cottage. If she talked to Sable, Sable and Margot are seen together at the Saloon some Friday evenings.

## 10 hearts: under the paint (her cottage, after hours; you must be dating)

**Trigger:** you enter her cottage between 8pm and midnight, dating, with 10+ hearts and the 8-heart event seen.

**Scene:**
- One of the old paintings is under her lamp, lit from behind. `$u` "Look. Hold it up to the light."
- Faint lines show through the paint: a drawing underneath, a figure the painter started and painted over. "They changed their mind. Right there, in the middle. Painters do. It's my favourite thing to find."
- `$7` "Everything I've ever fixed was someone else's mind, changing." beat. "I want to make something that's mine. I'm terrified it'll be awful."
- She sets a small blank canvas on the table, then a second one. `$l` "Will you paint with me? So if mine's awful, at least we'll both have one."
- **Choice:**
  - "Let's paint each other." → `$h` They paint each other, laughing at every stroke. (Reward: the two small portraits as furniture: "Margot, by You" and "You, by Margot".)
  - "Let's paint the window." → `$7` They paint the north window at night, side by side. (Reward: "North Window, by Margot and You".)
- The scene fades on the two canvases drying on the windowsill.

## 14 hearts: both names (married; the museum, evening)

**Trigger:** you're married, with 14 hearts, and you enter the museum between 5pm and 7pm.

**Scene:**
- Gunther has hung a single restored painting in the corner of the library. Margot stands in front of it, hands behind her back. `$u` "Read the card."
- The card: *"The [painting's title]. Unknown artist, c. 1890. Restored by Margot Pell and [you], [year]."* (The first old painting you restored, or the Old Mill if none.)
- `$l` "I've signed a thousand condition reports. I've never had my name on a card." beat. "Ours. Our names."
- Gunther: "It's the most popular painting in the museum." Margot: `$a` "It's the *only* painting in the museum, Gunther." Gunther: "And the most popular."
- `$h` "You don't make it new. You make it able to keep being old." She takes your hand. "Let's keep being old."

## Not a heart event: the test (the museum, any time once your mastery bar is full)

The Painting Mastery claim (`restoration_mechanics.md` §1), with Margot.

**Trigger:** talk to Margot at the museum with Painting at level 10 and the mastery bar full (15,000 XP past level 10).

**Scene:**
- `$u` "Your hands have been busy. I can tell from here. Sit."
- She sets a damaged old painting on the desk (one of hers from the city). "Restore it. Properly. Gold or better, and I'll sign the paper that says you're a conservator."
- The easel opens on the restoration. If you finish at gold or better: the vanilla mastery sparkle and sound; `$h` "Congratulations, conservator. Now you can do it yourself, and I can stop doing your homework." You receive the Master's Easel.
- Below gold: `$7` "Close. Rest your eyes. Come back tomorrow." You can try again the next day.

## Bouquet, pendant and marriage

**Bouquet** (8+ hearts): `$l` "Flowers. For me. I'm going to press one and keep it forever and you're not allowed to watch me do it."

**Mermaid's pendant** (10 hearts, dating): `$6` "...Yes. Yes! Sorry. Yes." beat. `$h` "I'm going to need a new card for the museum."

**The wedding:** as Sable's (its wedding script switches her to the wedding dress). Gunther and Sable in the front row; Julian, if he's been met, at the back, pretending not to cry.

**Married life:** she keeps her museum hours. Spouse room: her restoration table with the lamp and a painting under repair. Patio: a drying rack of small canvases: she's started painting her own. Sometimes she brings an old painting home to show you.
