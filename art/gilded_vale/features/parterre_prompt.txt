An old formal garden gone wild, painted into this Stardew Valley map in the game's own pixel art, the same top-down front camera, palette and grass as the surrounding map: a low crumbling grey stone wall with mossy capstones runs around the garden; inside, a symmetrical parterre of clipped box-hedge beds in geometric shapes, now overgrown, spilling with wildflowers and creeping vines; pale gravel paths between the beds; a gravel path leads from the cottage door at the top-left down into the garden, and the main walk runs left to right to a gap in the wall on the right side, where it meets the cobbled lane; three weathered marble statues on mossy plinths stand along the main walk (a dancer, a bust on a column, an abstract stone ring); an old stone birdbath in the middle. Seamless with the grass outside the wall.
